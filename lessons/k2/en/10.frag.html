K2 Lesson 10: Animals & Habitats

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Name 8 animals and their habitats (farm, sea, jungle).
  • Say "A ___ lives in the ___."

Vocab

  • fish / sea: "A fish lives in the sea."
  • cow / farm | tiger / jungle

Warm-up (5min)

Animal sound quiz — sound only, students guess animal.

Main Activity (20min): "Habitat Sort"

Three poster zones: Farm, Sea, Jungle. Sort animal cards. Each student places one card and says the full sentence.

Wrap-up (5min)

Favorite animal share: "I like ___. It lives in the ___."

Teacher Tips

  • Full sentence frame scaffolds grammar early.
  • Use photos, not cartoons, for habitat accuracy.
