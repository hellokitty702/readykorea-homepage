K2 Lesson 07: Guided Reading: ORT Stage 2

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Follow a Level 2 ORT book with teacher guidance.
  • Answer "Who?" and "What?" after each page.

Vocab

  • Title / page / character: Book orientation language.
  • Story-specific words from chosen ORT Stage 2 title.

Warm-up (5min)

Cover preview: "What do you see? Who is this?" Predict the story.

Main Activity (25min): "Guided Read (Small Groups)"

Groups of 4 with teacher. Echo read → choral read → student points and reads one word. Rotate groups. Use finger tracking.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw favorite scene — tell one sentence about it.

Teacher Tips

  • ORT Stage 2 = 1 sentence per page; perfect for K2 entry.
  • Never send home unreadable books — confidence first.
