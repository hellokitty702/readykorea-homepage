K2 Lesson 03: Phonics Groups 2-3: c/k & e

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Produce /k/ and /e/ sounds with Jolly actions.
  • Identify c/k at word start in cap, kit, cup.

Vocab

  • c/k — castanet click: "K-k-k"
  • e — egg chop: "E-e-e" short vowel.

Warm-up (5min)

Jolly Phonics video clip for c and e — full action copy.

Main Activity (20min): "Sound Sort Hoops"

Three hoops: /k/ | /e/ | other. Sort picture cards (cat, egg, sun, cup, bed). Say sound at start of each word.

Wrap-up (5min)

Air-write c and e while chanting sounds.

Teacher Tips

  • c and k make the same sound at start — teach as one sound family.
  • Short /e/ only; long vowels come in K3.
