K1 Lesson 10: Food & Fruits Taste Game

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Apple · Banana · Orange · Grape · Yummy · Pretend eat · /a/ apple · /b/ banana

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Fruit picture cards (6): apple · banana · orange · grape · strawberry · watermelon — photo + word, large
  • Taste-safe pretend fruit props (6): plastic or laminated fruit cutouts — NOT real food unless school policy allows
  • Yummy / Yucky face cards (2): thumbs up yummy · gentle yucky face — for taste reaction game
  • Phonics flashcards: apple picture + /a/ apple · banana picture + /b/ banana (review)
  • Plate mat (6): paper plate circles — one per child for pretend eating
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Hello / Goodbye cards: brief greeting from Lesson 02
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Optional: real taste-safe fruit slices in sealed cups — only with allergy list cleared by director

Learning Objectives

  • Students name six fruits: apple, banana, orange, grape, strawberry, watermelon.
  • Students hold a fruit card and pretend eat with "Yummy!" gesture — no real eating required.
  • Students respond to "Do you like ___?" with thumbs up or one word (yes / yummy).
  • Students participate in the Taste Game — pick a fruit card, mime bite, share reaction.
  • Students review /a/ (apple) and /b/ (banana) with picture cards and bite TPR.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Build food vocabulary safely with pretend play, then energize with fruit chorus before the Taste Game.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, preview fruit theme with pretend-only rule.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, friends! Hello! Welcome back. Sit on your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "Today we talk about fruit — yummy food!"
"Apple, banana, orange, grape. Pretend eat today — mime bite — no real food in mouth unless teacher gives OK."

Teacher · Door greeting script

"Hello, [name]! Welcome — today we taste yummy food with our imagination!"
"Find your spot. No real eating in class — pretend only. Both are fun!"
"[Name] is here — our fruit friends are waiting!"

Teacher · Fruit card peek

"Peek — apple!" (flash apple card) "Peek — banana!" (flash banana card)
"Fruit is for naming and pretending — gentle hands on cards. Cards stay in teacher basket until game time."
"Are you hungry for English? Yummy!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child puts pretend fruit in mouth and chews plastic, gentle redirect: "Pretend bite — mime only — lips together — yummy sound!"

If a child says real food is yucky rudely, model: "We say No thank you — quiet voice — or Yummy!"

If allergy concern arises, skip real taste entirely: "Today pretend only — 100% safe — still yummy fun!"

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — state pretend-eat rule before any fruit props appear.
Management Cue: Keep fruit props in closed basket until Step 3 — prevents early grabbing and mouthing.
Management Cue: Check allergy list before lesson — if any fruit allergy, avoid real food and matching picture triggers if needed.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Flash apple and banana cards — model pretend bite mime (≈1 min).
  4. State pretend-only rule clearly (≈30 sec).
  5. Preview Taste Game: "Pick fruit — pretend eat — Yummy!" (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + fruit word chorus with mime bite before individual Taste Game shares.

Teacher · Greeting + command review

"Hello, friends! How are you? I'm happy — and hungry! Yummy!"
"Look! Listen! Sit! Magic words — always!"

Teacher · Fruit word chorus

"Apple!" (mime bite — ah-ah-apple) "Banana!" (peel mime — ba-ba-banana)
"Orange!" (round hands) "Grape!" (small pop in mouth) "Yummy yummy!" (thumbs up)
"Your turn — copy my bite. Apple — chomp! Banana — peel peel — yum!"

Teacher · Do you like ___? drill

"Do you like apples?" (thumbs up) "Yes! Yummy!"
"Do you like bananas?" (mime peel) "Yummy!"
"Do you like purple grapes?" (pop pop) "Me too — yummy circle!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child shouts "Yucky!" at a classmate's favorite fruit, redirect: "Different likes — thumbs up or quiet hands — kind words."

If mime bite becomes hitting neighbor, reset: "Bite the air — bite your own pretend fruit — gentle."

If a child names candy instead of fruit, smile: "Candy later — today fruit — apple! Red apple!"

Management Cue: Model exaggerated mime bite on imaginary fruit in front of face — never toward another child.
Management Cue: Thumbs up / palms flat "no thank you" — two acceptable answers for Do you like ___? — no forced yum.
Management Cue: After fruit chorus, Listen card + 3-second wait — transition from loud yum to structured game.
  1. Hello + Look · Listen · Sit — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  2. Fruit word chorus with mime bite × six fruits (≈2 min).
  3. Do you like ___? — apple, banana, orange quick poll (≈1 min).
  4. Yummy face card model — thumbs up reaction (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "Time for Taste Game — pretend eat for real fun!"

Teacher · Transition to Main Activity

"Warm-up finished! You know apple, banana, orange, grape."
"Look at me. Listen. Sit. Quiet hands — pretend taste game is next!"
"Remember — Yummy! means we like it. Pretend bite — mmm — no real food today!"

Main Activity: Taste Game & /a/ /b/ Phonics Review (15 mins)

Purpose: Opening Up builds fruit vocabulary through pretend taste circle; Cheering Up reviews /a/ apple and /b/ banana with bite TPR. Pretend eat only unless director approves real taste.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — taste-safe fruit cards pretend eat: pick card, mime bite, say fruit name + Yummy!

Teacher · Taste Game setup

"Taste Game! I give you one fruit card and one plate. Hold your fruit. Pretend bite — chomp chomp — say the fruit name!"
"Then — Yummy! or No thank you — quiet. Show me your face."
"Friends watch — quiet hands — smile at yummy faces!"

Teacher · Model round

"Watch me. Apple card — chomp chomp — Apple! Yummy!" (thumbs up)
"Banana — peel peel — chomp — Banana! Yummy!"
"Your turn, Sora! Pick — bite — name — yummy face!"

Teacher · Taste reaction chorus

"When I pretend bite the apple — you copy — bite, chew, smile — Yummy!"
"Banana — peel motion — pretend peel — bite — Yummy! All together!"
"If you don't like it, gentle face — still say the fruit word — apple! banana!"

Teacher · Share rounds

"Round one — each child one fruit. Round two — swap cards — new fruit — new bite!"
"Round three — mystery bite! Close eyes — bite — guess — Apple? Yes!"
"Taste Game finished — cards in basket — plates stack here!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child bites real plastic prop, swap for laminated card: "Paper fruit — pretend chomp — no teeth on plastic."

If a child refuses to share, accept listen role: "You watch bites — guess the fruit — still part of game!"

If mystery round gets too silly (spitting sounds), reset: "Quiet chomp — lips together — Yummy voice only."

If real fruit is used and child has allergy, skip immediately — substitute card with their safe fruit only.

Management Cue: Pass cards plate-first, then fruit — structured handoff reduces dropped props at K1.
Management Cue: One speaker at a time during share — Listen card if two children chomp-talk over each other.
Management Cue: Collect cards immediately after each round — "Basket time" — before next round starts.
  1. Distribute plate + one fruit card per child (≈1 min).
  2. Teacher models pretend bite + Yummy for apple and banana (≈1 min).
  3. Round 1: each child bites, names fruit, shows reaction (≈3 min).
  4. Round 2: swap cards — new fruit mime (≈2 min).
  5. Round 3 (optional): eyes closed guess bite — teacher holds card to forehead (≈1 min).
  6. Collect cards and plates into basket (≈30 sec).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Phonics review /a/ apple and /b/ banana — picture cards + bite TPR + peer cheer.

Teacher · Review /a/ apple

"Apple! A says ah-ah-apple. Mouth open wide — ah-ah-ah. Apple — ah!"
"This is apple." (show apple card) "Bite TPR — chomp — ah — apple! Yummy ah!"
"Stand up in 3, 2, 1 — one pretend bite — ah! Sit down — quiet ah."

Teacher · Review /b/ banana + partner cheer

"Banana! B says buh-buh-banana. Lips together pop — buh-buh-buh. Banana — buh!"
"This is banana." (show banana card) "Peel and bite — buh — banana!"
"Partner cheer: you bite apple — ah! Friend bites banana — buh! Switch!"

Teacher · Praise frames

"Wow! I heard a-a-apple — ah sound!"
"Super banana sound — buh-buh!"
"Your apple bite face is perfect — ah!"
"High five for yummy sounds — buh — banana!"

Teacher · Bite TPR check

"Apple card — chomp — ah! Banana card — peel bite — buh!"
"Stand — apple bite — ah! Sit — banana bite — buh! In your spot."
"Last check — hold both cards — apple or banana? Listen and bite!"
Handling Mistakes:

If /a/ is /e/ ("epple"), model wide mouth: "Ah — apple — watch my big mouth."

If /b/ is /p/ ("pana"), hand on throat: "Buh has voice — buh — feel buzz — banana!"

If bite TPR becomes hitting, reset: "Bite the air — chomp — ah — gentle mime only."

If partner cheer gets messy, teacher pairs: "You are my apple — ah! I am banana — buh!"

Management Cue: Stand up in 3, 2, 1 / Sit down in 3, 2, 1 — one stand bite only per phonics round — no repeated jumping.
Management Cue: Shoulder partners for cheer — mime bite toward own face, not partner's face.
Management Cue: Label as review loudly: "We learned ah and buh before — today yummy review!" — builds confidence.
  1. Show apple card — model /a/ × 3, bite TPR × 3.
  2. Show banana card — model /b/ × 3, peel-bite TPR × 3.
  3. Alternating drill: teacher holds apple or banana — class bites + sound.
  4. Partner cheer: 30 seconds each side — apple ah / banana buh exchange.
  5. Whole-group check: apple card → ah + chomp; banana card → buh + peel bite.

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Close food play calmly, affirm pretend eating and naming, dismiss with Goodbye Song.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Purpose: Breathing, compliment, goodbye song, dismissal.

Teacher · Calm breathing

"Yummy time is finished. Sit on your spot. Hands in lap — empty plate — quiet tummy."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… ah, 1, 2, 3. Soft apple breath out."
"Again. In… 1, 2, 3. Out… buh… quiet banana breath. Tummy still — finished."

Teacher · Compliment circle

"Today you named fruit in our pretend taste game! You chomped apple and banana. You said ah and buh."
"I am proud of [name] for a brave yummy face. I am proud of [name] for kind listening."
"I am proud of [name] for gentle pretend bites. Everyone gets a sticker."

Teacher · Goodbye routine

"Stand up slowly. Stretch — big arms — then quiet body."
"Goodbye Song time. Wave your hands."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye, goodbye, see you again."
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone — see you again!"
"Line up at the door. High five — bye bye! Say apple and banana at home!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child keeps chomping during breathing, smile: "Tummy full of English — quiet ah breath — lips closed."

If child asks for real snack at door, follow school policy — do not promise food unless approved.

If line-up rush, pause: "Walking feet — same order — hungry friends wait together."

Management Cue: Fruit basket closed and away before breathing — visual signal that eating play is done.
Management Cue: Compliment 2–3 children by name — include one quiet listener during Taste Game.
Management Cue: Dismiss in table order — fruit sticker at door optional exit ritual.
  1. Two breath cycles with /a/ and /b/ exhale cues (≈1 min).
  2. Compliments + stickers — name 2–3 Taste Game moments (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye Song with individual name verse (≈2 min).
  4. Line up — high-five exit (≈1 min).
