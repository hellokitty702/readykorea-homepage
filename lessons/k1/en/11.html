K1 Lesson 11: Weather & Seasons

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Sun · Rain · Cloud · Hot · Cold · Weather chart · Dress mime · /s/ sun · /r/ rain

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Weather picture cards (5): sun · rain · cloud · snow · wind — photo + word, large
  • Weather chart (1): pocket chart or poster with Today slots — sun/rain/cloud icons attach with Velcro
  • Dress-up prop cards (6): hat · coat · boots · umbrella · sunglasses · scarf — for dress-for-weather mime
  • Season strip cards (4): spring · summer · fall · winter — simple icon + word
  • Phonics flashcards: sun picture + /s/ sun · rain picture + /r/ rain
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Hello / Goodbye cards: brief greeting from Lesson 02
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Optional: window check — real weather glance before chart update (30 sec)

Learning Objectives

  • Students name five weather words: sun, rain, cloud, snow, wind.
  • Students use hot and cold with gesture (fan face / hug self) for weather talk.
  • Students update a class weather chart — attach today's sun or rain icon together.
  • Students mime dress for weather — umbrella for rain, hat for sun — with prop cards.
  • Students hear and respond to /s/ (sun) and /r/ (rain) with picture cards and sky TPR.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Connect to real-world weather, preview vocabulary, then build sun/rain gestures before chart and dress mime activities.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, preview weather theme with window glance.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, friends! Hello! Welcome back. Sit on your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "Today we talk about weather — sun, rain, clouds!"
"Look at the window — what do you see? Sun? Cloud? Rain? We check together!"

Teacher · Door greeting script

"Hello, [name]! Welcome — look outside — what is the weather today?"
"Find your dot. Today we mime sun and rain with our whole body!"
"[Name] is here — our weather team is complete!"

Teacher · Weather card peek

"Peek — sun!" (flash sun card, arms round overhead) "Peek — rain!" (flash rain card, wiggle fingers down)
"Hot and cold — fan your face for hot — hug yourself for cold. Weather words help us dress!"
"Chart time comes later — listen first!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child shouts wrong weather ("Snow!" on sunny day), validate: "Snow is cold weather — today maybe sun — let's look again!"

If window distraction pulls group off circle, shorten glance: "Quick look — back to dot — eyes on me."

If a child uses L1 weather word only, echo in English: "Yes — sun! Sunny day!"

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — window glance max 30 seconds — then return to circle focus.
Management Cue: Weather chart visible but icons in teacher hand until Step 3 — prevents early Velcro grabbing.
Management Cue: Dress prop cards stay in basket until dress mime — avoids umbrella poking during Meeting Up.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Quick window weather glance — one word answer accepted (≈30 sec).
  4. Flash sun and rain cards — model sky gestures (≈1 min).
  5. Preview chart + dress mime: "We dress for weather later!" (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + weather gesture chorus — sun arms, rain fingers, hot/cold mime.

Teacher · Greeting + command review

"Hello, friends! How are you? I'm happy! What's the weather?"
"Look! Listen! Sit! Magic words — always!"

Teacher · Weather gesture chorus

"Sun!" (arms big circle overhead) "Rain!" (wiggle fingers down) "Cloud!" (arms fluffy)
"Hot!" (fan face) "Cold!" (hug self, shiver) "Wind!" (arms sway)
"Your turn — copy my sky. Sun — big! Rain — drip drip drip!"

Teacher · Hot / cold contrast

"Sun day — HOT — fan fan fan!"
"Rain day — COLD — brrr — hug!"
"Show me hot — show me cold — fast switch — hot — cold — sit!"
Handling Mistakes:

If rain gesture becomes hitting neighbor's head, reset: "Rain falls on YOU — wiggle fingers on your own head — gentle drip."

If hot/cold switch causes standing chaos, add Sit between: "Hot — Sit — Cold — Sit — controlled switch."

If a child says "Thunder!", redirect: "Loud sky! Today — sun, rain, cloud — pick one gesture."

Management Cue: Model all weather gestures on self first — sun arms overhead, rain fingers on own shoulders — never on another child.
Management Cue: Hot/cold switch max 3 times — then Sit + Listen card — prevents overheated motor play before chart work.
Management Cue: Half-class gesture split if tight space: "This side sun — that side rain!" then swap.
  1. Hello + Look · Listen · Sit — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  2. Weather gesture chorus — sun, rain, cloud, wind × 1 pass (≈2 min).
  3. Hot / cold mime switch × 3 with Sit between (≈1 min).
  4. Listen card + 3-second wait (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "You know the sky — weather chart time!"

Teacher · Transition to Main Activity

"Warm-up finished! You know sunny, rainy, cloudy, snowy, hot, cold."
"Look at me. Listen. Sit. Weather chart mime is next — follow my hands!"
"Point to the chart — say the weather word — then mime with your body!"

Main Activity: Weather Chart & Dress Mime + /s/ /r/ Phonics (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up builds weather literacy through chart update and dress-for-weather mime; Cheering Up locks /s/ sun and /r/ rain through picture cards and sky TPR.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — weather chart update + dress for weather mime with prop cards.

Teacher · Weather chart setup

"Weather chart! What is today? Sun or rain or cloud? Help me choose the icon!"
"[Child name] puts the sun on the chart — sticky Velcro — Today is sunny!"
"Everyone points — Sun! Hot! Fan your face!"

Teacher · Dress for weather mime

"Dress for weather! If rain — umbrella!" (mime hold umbrella prop card) "If sun — hat and sunglasses!"
"If cold rain — coat and boots! Mime put on — stomp stomp — boots!"
"I say weather — you dress mime. Ready — Rain! (class mimes umbrella) Sun! (hat + sun arms)"

Teacher · Season bridge script

"Spring — flowers grow — arms up slowly. Summer — hot — fan your face!"
"Fall — leaves fall — hands drift down. Winter — cold — hug yourself — brrr!"
"Weather and seasons go together — sunny summer — rainy spring — listen and mime!"

Teacher · Chart + mime rounds

"Round one — chart update + group dress mime for today's weather."
"Round two — mystery weather! I flash a card — rain — dress fast — umbrella!"
"Round three — partner show: show your prop card — Sun — hat! Rain — umbrella! Chart finished!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child fights over Velcro icon, use helper role: "You pass the sun to Minho — helper job — important!"

If dress mime uses props as weapons (umbrella poke), reset: "Prop flat on shoulder — mime only — gentle weather dress."

If chart word is too hard, accept gesture: "Point to sun icon — thumbs up — perfect weather answer!"

If real window weather differs from chart vote, note both: "Window says cloud — chart says rain — both OK — English words!"

Management Cue: One chart updater per day — rotate helper weekly — others point and chant weather word.
Management Cue: Prop cards are flat laminate only — no open umbrellas in classroom if safety policy restricts.
Management Cue: Mystery weather round — in-place mime only — no running to dress-up corner at K1.
  1. Group votes today's weather — one child attaches chart icon (≈1 min).
  2. Teacher models dress mime for today's weather with props (≈1 min).
  3. Round 1: teacher calls sun/rain/cloud — class dress mimes (≈2 min).
  4. Round 2: mystery weather flash cards — fast dress mime (≈2 min).
  5. Round 3 (optional): partners show prop + weather word (≈1 min).
  6. Props in basket — chart stays visible on wall (≈30 sec).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Phonics /s/ sun and /r/ rain — picture cards + sky TPR + peer cheer.

Teacher · Introduce /s/ sun

"Sun starts with S! S says s-s-sun. Teeth together — sss-sss-sss. Sun — s-s-sun!"
"This is sun." (show sun card) "Sun TPR — arms big circle — sss — sun! Hot — fan!"
"Stand up in 3, 2, 1 — sun arms — sss! Sit down — quiet sss."

Teacher · Introduce /r/ rain + partner cheer

"Rain starts with R! R says r-r-rain. Lip buzz — rrr-rrr-rrr. Rain — r-r-rain!"
"This is rain." (show rain card) "Rain TPR — wiggle fingers down — rrr — rain! Drip drip!"
"Partner cheer: you are sun — sss arms! Friend is rain — rrr fingers! Switch!"

Teacher · Praise frames

"Wow! I heard sss — sun sound!"
"Super rain sound — rrr-rrr!"
"Your sun arms are perfect — sss — bright!"
"High five for rain fingers — rrr — pitter patter!"

Teacher · Sky TPR check

"Sun card — big arms — sss! Rain card — finger drip — rrr!"
"Stand — sun — sss! Sit — rain — rrr on your lap! Stay in spot."
"Last check — hold both cards — sun or rain? Listen and move!"
Handling Mistakes:

If /s/ has voice ("zun"), model teeth: "Snake sound — sss — no voice buzz — sun!"

If /r/ is /w/ ("wain"), accept at K1: "Great rain fingers! Rrr — lip buzz — rain!"

If sun arms hit neighbor, reset: "Sun above YOUR head — big circle — own sky only."

If partner cheer overlaps, assign sun side / rain side of circle before cheer starts.

Management Cue: Stand up in 3, 2, 1 / Sit down in 3, 2, 1 — sun stand only once per phonics block.
Management Cue: Shoulder partners for cheer — sun arms stay overhead, rain fingers on own shoulders.
Management Cue: Connect phonics to chart: point at wall chart sun while doing sss — links classroom anchor to sound.
  1. Show sun card — model /s/ × 3, sun arms TPR × 3.
  2. Show rain card — model /r/ × 3, rain finger TPR × 3.
  3. Alternating drill: teacher holds sun or rain card — class responds with gesture + sound.
  4. Partner cheer: 30 seconds each side — sun sss / rain rrr exchange.
  5. Whole-group check: sun card → sss + arms; rain card → rrr + finger drip.

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Calm after sky gestures, affirm chart helper and dress mime, dismiss with Goodbye Song.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Purpose: Breathing, compliment, goodbye song, dismissal.

Teacher · Calm breathing

"Weather time is finished. Sit on your spot. Hands in lap — quiet sky."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… sss, 1, 2, 3. Soft sun breath out."
"Again. In… 1, 2, 3. Out… rrr… quiet rain breath. Cloud still — finished."

Teacher · Compliment circle

"Today you read the weather chart! You mimed sun and rain. You made sss and rrr sounds."
"I am proud of [name] for chart helper. I am proud of [name] for gentle umbrella mime."
"I am proud of [name] for bright sun arms. Everyone gets a sticker."

Teacher · Goodbye routine

"Stand up slowly. Stretch — reach high — then still body."
"Goodbye Song time. Wave your hands."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye, goodbye, see you again."
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone — see you again!"
"Line up at the door. High five — bye bye! Mime sun and rain at home!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child keeps rain fingers on neighbor during breathing, gentle hands: "Rain on your lap — drip — quiet sss."

If child upset about wrong chart vote, validate: "Weather changes — tomorrow new icon — you did great English!"

If line-up rush, pause song: "Walking feet — cloud slow — you can do it."

Management Cue: Compliment chart helper by name daily — builds rotation buy-in for future lessons.
Management Cue: Props in basket before breathing — dress mime is closed.
Management Cue: Dismiss in table order — point at chart on way out: "Tomorrow we check weather again!"
  1. Two breath cycles with /s/ and /r/ exhale cues (≈1 min).
  2. Compliments + stickers — name 2–3 weather moments (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye Song with individual name verse (≈2 min).
  4. Line up — high-five exit (≈1 min).
