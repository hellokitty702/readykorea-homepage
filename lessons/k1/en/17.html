K1 Lesson 17: Songs & Goodbye Routine

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Hello Song · Goodbye Song · Cleanup Song · Song Medley · Rhythm Instruments · Exit Ritual

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Hello Song audio: fast + slow versions (30–45 sec each)
  • Goodbye Song audio: primary class version with name verse space
  • Cleanup Song audio: "Clean up, clean up, everybody everywhere"
  • Rhythm instruments (class set): shakers · tambourines · rhythm sticks — 1 per child max
  • Instrument basket (1): labeled return bin for Cheering Up
  • Goodbye ritual props: high-five glove · line-up tape · door poster "See you again!"
  • Song menu cards (3): Hello · Cleanup · Goodbye — picture icons
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Optional: parent permission form if recording medley for families

Learning Objectives

  • Students sing Hello, Cleanup, and Goodbye songs from memory with gestures.
  • Students perform a full song medley — hello through goodbye — without stopping.
  • Students play rhythm instruments in time with song beats — stop when music stops.
  • Students complete the end-of-class goodbye ritual independently: stand, song, line up, high-five.
  • Students understand the goodbye ritual reduces separation anxiety through predictable closure.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Settle into circle, vote on Hello song version, preview full medley arc before rhythm instrument work.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, singers! Hello! Same safe circle — sit on your dot."
"Look · Listen · Sit. Today is song day — Hello, cleanup, Goodbye — our class music story!"
"Every day we end the same way — Goodbye Song — so your heart knows what comes next."

Teacher · Song menu vote

"Song menu! Hello song — fast or slow? Show thumbs — fast! Slow! I hear fast wins!"
"Cleanup song later. Goodbye song last — always last — our special ending."
"Three song cards — Hello — Cleanup — Goodbye — in order — like a story!"

Teacher · Medley arc preview

"Today we sing all three — one medley — no stopping — Hello to Goodbye — a music journey!"
"Instruments later — rhythm cheer — but first — our voices — beautiful singers!"
Handling Mistakes:

If vote becomes shouting, use hand vote only — "Hands up for fast — hands up for slow."

If a child cries at "goodbye" preview, reassure: "Goodbye means see you again — not forever!"

Management Cue: Instruments in basket — visible but locked until Step 4 — prevents early shaking.
  1. Door greeting + circle settle with Look · Listen · Sit.
  2. Song menu vote — Hello fast vs slow (≈1 min).
  3. Preview medley arc on song menu cards — Hello → lesson → cleanup → goodbye (≈1 min).
  4. Emphasize goodbye ritual — "Same ending every day" (≈1 min).
  5. Hello wave to neighbor (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · Hello song warm-up

"Fast Hello Song — stand up — wave — sing!" (play 30 sec clip)
"Hello, hello, how are you? I'm happy — thumbs up! Sit down."
"Again — slow Hello — gentle wave — hello, hello — sit."

Teacher · Goodbye gesture preview

"Goodbye warm-up — wave slow — bye bye — see you again!"
"Hands on heart — friends — teacher — see you again. This is our goodbye feeling."

Teacher · Cleanup chant preview

"Cleanup chant preview — Clean up, clean up — hands pretend pick up — everybody everywhere!"
"Pretend tidy — blocks in the basket — books on the shelf — clean up, clean up!"

Teacher · Three-song gesture chain

"Stand — Hello wave! Pretend tidy — cleanup hands! Slow wave — goodbye feeling — sit!"
"That is our medley story — Hello — tidy — Goodbye — remember the order!"
Handling Mistakes:

If children run during Hello stand-up, reset: "Stand in 3-2-1 on your dot — feet stay on the spot."

If goodbye wave hits neighbors, model small wave: "Gentle wave — like a butterfly wing."

Management Cue: Stand/sit counts every time — "Stand 3-2-1" / "Sit 3-2-1" — non-negotiable song transitions.
  1. Look · Listen · Sit — 1 round (≈30 sec).
  2. Fast Hello Song clip with stand-wave-sit (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye gesture preview — slow wave + heart hands (≈1 min).
  4. Cleanup chant — seated pretend tidy motion (≈1 min).
  5. Three-song gesture chain — stand/tidy/wave (≈1 min).

Main Activity: Song Medley & Rhythm Instruments (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up runs the full hello-to-goodbye medley twice; Cheering Up adds rhythm instruments with strict stop-when-I-stop rules. Goodbye ritual is the lesson's emotional anchor — never rush or skip.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Teacher · Medley setup

"Showtime medley! We do not stop — four parts — one story. Ready — stand!"
"Part one — Hello Song — wave — sing — I'm happy!"
"Part two — mini chant — Look, Listen, Sit — clap clap!"
"Part three — Cleanup Song — pretend tidy — clean up everybody!"
"Part four — Goodbye Song — slow wave — goodbye [name] — see you again! Sit."

Teacher · Medley run-through — teacher led

"Run one — I lead every word. Follow my hands — Hello wave — cleanup tidy — goodbye wave."
"No stopping between parts — breathe — next part — music story flows!"

Teacher · Line-up practice

"Line up practice — quiet feet — stand at the circle edge — high-five at the door — Bye, teacher!"
"Child says — Bye, teacher! Teacher says — See you again! Practice now — walking feet!"

Teacher · Medley run-through — child-led goodbye

"Run two — you lead Goodbye names — I help. Same order — Hello — chant — cleanup — goodbye."
"Goodbye is always last. Same words — same wave — every single day."
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name] — every friend — no skipping — see you again!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child sits during Hello stand-up, tap their spot: "Stand with us — sit at Goodbye — that's the pattern."

If cleanup becomes throwing toys, pause medley: "Pretend tidy only today — hands in the air — pick up imaginary blocks."

If goodbye names are skipped, slow down: "Every friend gets a name — goodbye Minho — goodbye Sora — everyone."

Management Cue: Medley cue cards on floor in sequence — visual roadmap prevents drift between songs.
  1. Teacher-led medley run 1 — full four-part sequence (≈3 min).
  2. Line-up practice at circle edge — quiet feet — high-five model (≈1 min).
  3. Child-led Goodbye names run 2 — teacher supports shy singers (≈3 min).
  4. Return to circle — "Medley finished — instruments next!"

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Teacher · Instrument rules

"Instrument cheer! One instrument each — from the basket — no trading yet."
"When I sing — you shake. When I stop — freeze — instruments quiet on the floor."
"Rule one — play with the song. Rule two — stop when I stop. Rule three — return to basket."

Teacher · Freeze practice without song

"Practice first — shake shake shake — stop! Freeze! Instruments down — wonderful stopping!"
"Again — shake — stop! If you cannot stop — clap only — we try shakers again."

Teacher · Hello rhythm round

"Hello beat — shake shake shake — stop! Freeze! Shake with the hello song — happy rhythm!"
"Fast hello — fast shake — slow hello — slow shake — stop!"

Teacher · Goodbye rhythm round

"Goodbye beat — slow shake — wave — goodbye goodbye — stop! Instruments down."
"Gentle goodbye shake — like a lullaby — wave — stop — peaceful music!"

Teacher · Final three-song rhythm cheer

"Final cheer — Hello fast shake — Cleanup tap tap — Goodbye slow shake — freeze — basket!"
"All instruments in the basket — clap once — music heroes — sit on your spot!"
Handling Mistakes:

If instruments become weapons, collect immediately: "Instruments rest — clap only — we try again tomorrow."

If a child cannot stop on signal, practice freeze without instruments first — "Freeze!" — then retry with shakers.

If trading causes tears, assign instruments by table color — no swaps this lesson.

Management Cue: "Stop" signal = raised hand + sharp inhale — same signal every music lesson.
  1. Instrument rules demo — shake + freeze × 3 without song (≈1 min).
  2. Hello beat — shake with song clip — stop signal × 2 (≈2 min).
  3. Goodbye beat — slow shake + wave — stop signal × 2 (≈2 min).
  4. Final three-song rhythm cheer — Hello → cleanup tap → goodbye shake (≈1 min).
  5. All instruments in basket — children clap once — "Music heroes!"

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Full goodbye ritual performance — breathing, compliment, real dismissal with high-five exit. This IS the lesson's core outcome.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Teacher · Breathing before goodbye

"Hands on tummy. Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Out… goodbye breath — slow wave."
"You sang Hello. You played instruments. You know our goodbye — I am proud."

Teacher · Compliment singers

"I am proud of [name] for freezing on stop. I am proud of [name] for brave goodbye wave."
"Stickers for singers — line up — quiet feet — real goodbye time!"

Teacher · Full goodbye ritual — real dismissal

"Stand up — line up — quiet feet. Goodbye Song — final version — everyone's name."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone!"
"High-five at the door. Student says: Bye, teacher! Teacher says: See you again!"
"Same goodbye tomorrow — same goodbye every day — you are safe."
Handling Mistakes:

If a child clings at door, kneel: "Goodbye song finished — see you again — high-five?" Never pull away from parents.

If line-up dissolves, restart calmly: "Line up practice again — you are my line leader — quiet feet."

Management Cue: Goodbye ritual identical every lesson hereafter — zero variation for 2 weeks minimum.
  1. One breath cycle + compliment (≈1 min).
  2. Real line-up + full Goodbye Song with every name (≈2 min).
  3. High-five exit ritual — child says "Bye, teacher!" (≈1 min).
  4. Sticker at door for singers who completed ritual (≈1 min).
