K1 Lesson 18: K1 Final Review

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Review Stations · Commands · Colors & Numbers · Animals · Phonics · K1 Champion Circle · Certificate · K2 Preview

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Station 1 — Commands: Look · Listen · Sit · Stand · Come here cards + Teacher Says paddle
  • Station 2 — Colors & Numbers: red/blue/yellow/green swatches + number cards 1–10 + match mat
  • Station 3 — Animals: cow · pig · duck · sheep · horse · chicken cards + animal headbands (×6)
  • Station 4 — Phonics: Group 1 letter cards s-a-t-i-p-n + action icon strip + blend cards sat/pin/pan
  • Station cone markers (4): labeled Commands · Colors+Numbers · Animals · Phonics
  • Rotation bell or chime (1): 90-second station signal
  • K1 Champion crown (1): paper crown for Cheering Up circle
  • Completion certificates (1 per child): "K1 Champion" with name line + date
  • K2 preview poster (1): "Next Stop — K2!" with simple icons
  • Group photo backdrop (optional): K1 banner if school permits photos
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child + certificate sticker seal
  • Hello / Goodbye song audio: for warm-up and wrap-up ritual

Learning Objectives

  • Students review commands (Look, Listen, Sit, Stand, Come here) through Teacher Says at Station 1.
  • Students review colors and numbers 1–10 through card matching at Station 2.
  • Students review farm animals and sounds through TPR at Station 3.
  • Students review Phonics Group 1 (s-a-t-i-p-n) actions and blends at Station 4.
  • Students participate in the K1 Champion Circle — celebrate effort, receive certificate, preview K2.
  • Students demonstrate K1 readiness through play-based stations — no formal written test.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Celebrate K1 finale energy safely, preview four review stations, run K1 Champion Hello with every name before station rotations.

"Look · Listen · Sit. Today we play four review stations — commands, colors, numbers, animals, phonics!"
"No test — only play — you show everything you learned. Are you ready?"

Teacher · Station preview — four cones

"Four stations around the room — orange cone Commands — blue cone Colors — green cone Animals — purple cone Phonics!"
"Small groups rotate — bell rings — next station. Walking feet — team review!"
"At the end — K1 Champion Circle — certificate — Next stop K2!"

Teacher · Certificate & K2 preview tease

"Everyone earns a certificate today — K1 Champion — your name — a golden sticker seal!"
"And a surprise — K2 preview poster — bigger adventures — but first — we celebrate K1!"

Teacher · Emotional reassurance

"Last K1 lesson means you grew — not goodbye forever — see you in K2 — same school — same friends!"
"Big feelings are OK — I am here — safe circle — champions together!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child cries at "last lesson," reassure: "K1 finished — K2 adventure next — you are growing!"

If children run to stations early, block path: "Stations wait — circle first — K1 Champion Hello!"

Management Cue: Station materials covered with cloths until rotation begins — prevents early exploration.
Management Cue: Post rotation chart with group colors — children find their color group instantly.
  1. Door greeting — extra celebratory Hello to each child.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit — 3-second settle.
  3. Point to four station cones — name each theme (≈1 min).
  4. Show certificate sample — "Everyone earns one today!"
  5. Preview K2 poster briefly — "Next stop — K2!"

Main Activity: Review Stations & K1 Champion Circle (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up runs four mini review stations (~2 min each per group, two rotation passes); Cheering Up is the K1 Champion Circle with whole-group cheer. Observe participation and TPR accuracy only — no written test.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — Four review stations in small-group rotation: commands, colors+numbers, animals, phonics.

Station 1 · Commands — Teacher Says (≈2 min per group)

Teacher · Station 1 setup & rules

"Station 1 — Commands! Orange cone — Teacher Says rules — I say Teacher Says — you do it. No Teacher Says — freeze!"
"Command cards on the table — Look — Listen — Sit — Stand — Come here — pictures help you!"
"Round one — easy — always Teacher Says. Round two — freeze trick — listen carefully!"

Teacher · Station 1 — command rounds

"Teacher Says — Look! (point eyes) Teacher Says — Listen! (hands on ears) Teacher Says — Sit!"
"Look! (freeze — no Teacher Says) Uh oh — freeze! Wonderful listening! Teacher Says — Stand!"
"Teacher Says — Come here! (walking feet to cone) Teacher Says — Sit! Freeze round — Jump! Freeze — no Teacher Says — champions!"

Teacher · Station 1 — close & rotate

"Commands finished — high-five — orange cone done — bell rings — walking feet — next station!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child moves on freeze round, smile: "Freeze champion — watch friends — try again next round."

If Come here causes running, clarify: "Walking feet — come here — stop at the cone."

Management Cue: Station leader uses command cards face-up as visual support — reduces verbal overload.
  1. Model Teacher Says vs freeze — 2 practice rounds.
  2. Full command set: Look · Listen · Sit · Stand · Come here × 2 passes.
  3. Freeze trick round × 1 — celebrate careful listeners.
  4. Bell — rotate to Station 2.

Station 2 · Colors & Numbers — Card Match (≈2 min per group)

Teacher · Station 2 setup

"Station 2 — Colors and numbers! Blue cone — match red card to red spot — number 5 to five dots!"
"Color zone on the left — number zone on the right — don't mix — team matching!"

Teacher · Station 2 — color match

"Touch red — say red! Touch blue — say blue! Touch yellow — say yellow! Touch green — say green!"
"Hold up red — show me — red! Swap cards — match every color — color champions!"

Teacher · Station 2 — number match

"Numbers — hold up 1 — say one! 2 — two! 3 — three! Match to dot sets — count together!"
"Stretch to 10 if ready — 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 — or stop at 5 — K1 heroes either way!"
"Quick cheer — touch red! Hold 7! Touch blue! Hold 3! — fast commands — wonderful!"

Teacher · Station 2 — close & rotate

"Colors and numbers finished — blue cone done — bell rings — walking feet — animal station next!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child matches by shape not color, hold swatch to object: "Same color — red — both red."

If counting skips a number, chant together slowly 1–5 before 6–10.

Management Cue: Match mat has color dots AND number dots — separate zones prevent card mixing.
  1. Color match — 4 swatches to 4 zones — choral name each.
  2. Number match — cards 1–10 to dot sets — stop at 5 for K1, stretch to 10 if ready.
  3. Quick combined call: "Touch red! Hold 7!" — 4 commands.
  4. Bell — rotate to Station 3.

Station 3 · Animals — Sound & TPR (≈2 min per group)

Teacher · Station 3 setup

"Station 3 — Farm animals! Green cone — pick a card — make the sound — do the motion!"
"Six animals — cow, pig, duck, sheep, horse, chicken — from Lesson 06 — you remember!"

Teacher · Station 3 — animal sounds

"Cow — moo — horns on head! Pig — oink — snout with hands! Duck — quack — flap wings!"
"Sheep — baa — fluffy arms! Horse — neigh — gallop feet! Chicken — cluck — wings tucked!"

Teacher · Station 3 — "The ___ says ___"

"The cow says… moo! The duck says… quack! I am a sheep — baa! I am a horse — neigh!"
"Group animal chorus — all together — moo — oink — quack — baa — neigh — cluck — 10 seconds — animal orchestra!"

Teacher · Station 3 — close & rotate

"Animals finished — green cone done — bell rings — walking feet — phonics station — last one!"
Handling Mistakes:

Accept sound-only if word is hard — "Great moo! I am a cow — moo — perfect."

If headbands slip, fix and continue — optional for station speed.

Management Cue: Animal cards stay at station — headbands optional quick wear — don't let dress-up slow rotation.
  1. Each child picks one animal card — sound + gesture.
  2. "The ___ says ___" — teacher prompts × 6 animals.
  3. Group animal chorus — moo/oink/quack/baa/neigh/cluck — 10 seconds.
  4. Bell — rotate to Station 4.

Station 4 · Phonics — Group 1 Action Flash (≈2 min per group)

Teacher · Station 4 setup

"Station 4 — Phonics Group 1! Purple cone — flash a letter — do the action — say the sound!"
"Action icon strip on the table — if you forget — look at the picture — snake, apple, tap, insect, puff, net!"

Teacher · Station 4 — six-sound flash

"S — snake — sss! A — apple — a-a-a! T — tap — t-t-t! I — insect — i-i-i! P — puff — p-p-p! N — net — n-n-n!"
"Faster — flash random — p — s — n — a — t — i — you remember every action — Group 1 masters!"

Teacher · Station 4 — blend cheer

"Blend cheer — sat! Pin! Pan! — slow… fast! Slide your finger like the blending rail — sat!"
"Six-sound chain — stand up — sss-a-t-i-p-n — sit down — Group 1 complete — phonics champions!"

Teacher · Station 4 — close & return

"Phonics finished — purple cone done — all four stations — walking feet — back to the big circle!"
Handling Mistakes:

If blend is too hard, accept actions only: "Wonderful snake — sss — K1 champion sound!"

If child confuses letters, show picture cue side — action first, always.

Management Cue: Action icon strip on station table — children self-check if they forget a motion.
  1. Flash s-a-t-i-p-n — group action response × 1 pass.
  2. Choral blend: sat · pin · pan — teacher leads slide motion.
  3. Six-sound chain stand-up — sit down — "Group 1 masters!"
  4. Bell — return to circle for K1 Champion Circle.

Teacher · Rotation close — all stations done

"Stations finished! Clipboards down — cones stay — back to the big circle — K1 Champion Circle next!"
"You reviewed commands, colors, numbers, animals, AND phonics — four stations — four cheers — incredible!"
Management Cue: Two adults minimum for rotations — one floats, one stays at circle with waiting group.
Management Cue: Single pass (~2 min × 4 stations = 8 min) if class is large — quality over double rotation.

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: K1 Champion Circle — whole-group cheer celebrating every child's K1 journey.

Teacher · K1 Champion Circle setup

"K1 Champion Circle! Stand up — arms up — you finished K1! Champion cheer!"
"Look — Listen — Sit — red blue — moo quack — sss a-a-a — K1 CHAMPIONS!"
"Again — louder — K1 CHAMPIONS! One more — biggest — K1 CHAMPIONS!"

Teacher · Partner high-five cheer

"Turn to your neighbor — high-five — say — You are a champion! Switch — other neighbor — You are a champion!"
"Look at me — I say — You are ALL champions — arms up — yay!"

Teacher · Crown pass

"Champion crown passes — wear it — wave — pass on — every friend wears the crown!"
"Touch the crown gently — three seconds — wave — pass clockwise — no pulling!"
"Last friend — crown to teacher — we did it — every champion wore the crown!"

Teacher · Group photo & calm transition

"Big smile — K1 champions — photo time!" (if permitted) "Cheese — champions!"
"Sit down — certificates coming — calm happy hearts — almost party time!"
Handling Mistakes:

If crown pass causes grabbing, teacher holds crown: "Touch the crown — don't pull — pass gentle."

If photo triggers shyness, opt-out spot with sticker — no forced smiles.

Management Cue: Champion cheer chant on poster — same words every year — builds school tradition.
Management Cue: Crown pass clockwise only — predictable direction prevents chaos.
  1. Whole-group K1 Champion cheer × 3 — commands/colors/animals/phonics mashup (≈2 min).
  2. Partner high-five — "You are a champion!" (≈1 min).
  3. Crown pass — each child wears crown 3 seconds (≈2 min).
  4. Optional group photo (≈1 min).
  5. Sit — hands in lap — "Certificates in wrap-up — almost party time!"

Management Cue: Certificates pre-written — ceremony is hand-off + sticker seal only — keeps wrap-up under 5 min.
Management Cue: Celebrate effort loudly — this sets tone for the whole 9-year roadmap.
Management Cue: Parents invited to door only for certificate hand-off if school policy allows — coordinate beforehand.
