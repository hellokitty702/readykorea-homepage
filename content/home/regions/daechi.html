Overview

Daechi-dong is the most concentrated English-academy neighborhood on the planet. If you\'re hired here, the apartment is often yours, the school is a 5-minute walk, and parents pay tuition that funds half the building.

Gangnam Station is 10 min away. Hongdae 35–40 min. Incheon Airport 80–100 min. ReadyKorea pre-vets every Daechi position and checks the apartment in person before you sign.

    Expat tips

    1. Apartment walkability is non-negotiable. We check it on foot — 6 m from school is the Daechi standard.
    2. Ask about gym discounts Day 1. Daechi hagwons often negotiate with nearby chains (₩70K→₩50K).
    3. Costco Yangjae monthly. 15 min taxi — ₩38,500 membership pays for itself in one trip.
    4. Competition is fierce. Schools replace fast if performance slips — treat Daechi as a proving ground.
    5. Use Papago at local clinics. International hospitals 20 min away for serious issues; Papago works for routine care.
