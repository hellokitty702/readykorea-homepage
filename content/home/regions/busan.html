Overview

Beach lifestyle with honest tradeoffs — from our Haeundae forum guide.

Haeundae (해운대) is Busan\'s most expat-friendly neighborhood — 2 km of beach, Marine City skyline, and a surf community at nearby Songjeong. Teachers here save less than Seoul counterparts but gain weekend hiking, swimming (June–September), and a calmer daily rhythm.

Real talk: fewer hagwon positions than Seoul, Busan dialect (사투리) takes adjustment, and Seoul trips cost ₩60K+ each way on KTX. Best for teachers who\'d rather live by the ocean than maximize monthly savings.

    Expat tips

    Five rules from 사장님 (John) — placing ~15 teachers in Busan per year.

    1. Accept the savings gap upfront. You\'ll likely save ₩400K–800K less per month than Daechi — budget for lifestyle, not max remittance.
    2. Learn Busan basics, not just Seoul Korean. Local dialect throws off Papago. Co-teachers appreciate any Busan phrases you try.
    3. Plan Seoul trips monthly, not weekly. KTX is ₩60K+ each way — batch errands and friend visits into one weekend.
    4. Pick housing by Line 2 proximity. Haeundae ↔ Seomyeon in 30 min on one line — don\'t sign a lease 2 bus transfers from your hagwon.
    5. Use the beach as decompression. Winter wind is real, but year-round boardwalk runs and Jangsan hiking prevent Seoul-style burnout.
