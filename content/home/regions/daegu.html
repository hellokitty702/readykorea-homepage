Overview

Daegu (대구) is Korea\'s third-largest metro and the main EPIK destination in the southeast — public schools, fewer hagwon listings than Seoul, but a loyal expat community and genuinely lower monthly burn. Teachers land here for Suseong and Dongseongno corridors, Kyungpook National University adjacency, and savings that actually stick.

Best for: EPIK-first teachers, slow-lane Korea, max savings. Tradeoff: fewer international groceries, Busan 50 min by train, Seoul KTX ~1h 40m — batch big-city trips.

    Expat tips

    1. EPIK placements dominate. Public school hours (8:30–4:30) differ from hagwon — read contract type before comparing salaries to Seoul.
    2. Summer is real. Daegu hits Korea\'s highest summer temps — budget AC electricity and plan indoor hagwon breaks.
    3. Kyungpook National Univ Hospital for serious care — listed in our medical guide; Papago works for local clinics.
    4. Busan is 50 min on KTX. Batch beach weekends — cheaper than living Busan with Daegu salary math.
    5. Smaller expat pool = faster friendships. Join Daegu Facebook groups Day 1 — teachers coordinate trips and Costco Busan runs.
