K2 — Ages 4-5

Core Competency: Phonics Group 2-3, CVC blending, and basic sentences.
18-unit curriculum for Pre-Starters.

K2 Lesson 01: CVC Blending

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Blend 3-letter CVC words orally (cat, pin, mat).
  • Match spoken words to picture cards.

Vocab

  • cat / pin / mat: Segment then blend: "/c/ /a/ /t/ — cat!"
  • Blend: "Put the sounds together."

Warm-up (5min)

Review Jolly Phonics Group 1 actions (s, a, t, i, p, n) — quick chorus.

Main Activity (20min): "Sound-Blending Relay"

Teacher holds letter cards c-a-t. Class blends. Teams race to pick the matching picture. Rotate through pin, mat, sat, tap.

Wrap-up (5min)

Students draw one CVC word and label with teacher help.

Teacher Tips

  • Stretch each sound 2 seconds before blending — don't rush.
  • If blending fails, return to first sound only; build back up.

K2 Lesson 02: Classroom Command Loop (TPR)

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Follow 2-step classroom commands (Stand up and clap).
  • Use Open, Close, Point, Give me in full phrases.

Vocab

  • Stand up / Sit down: Full-body response.
  • Open / Close: "Open your book." "Close the door."

Warm-up (5min)

Command chain from K1 review: Look, Listen, Sit — then add Stand up.

Main Activity (20min): "Command Loop"

Teacher calls a loop: "Stand up! Clap! Sit down! Open your book!" Students repeat the full loop twice without stopping. Pairs lead one command each.

Wrap-up (5min)

Exit ticket: perform 3 commands in order for a sticker.

Teacher Tips

  • K2 can handle 2-step commands — K1 could not.
  • Model first, then silent gesture only.

K2 Lesson 03: Phonics Groups 2-3: c/k & e

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Produce /k/ and /e/ sounds with Jolly actions.
  • Identify c/k at word start in cap, kit, cup.

Vocab

  • c/k — castanet click: "K-k-k"
  • e — egg chop: "E-e-e" short vowel.

Warm-up (5min)

Jolly Phonics video clip for c and e — full action copy.

Main Activity (20min): "Sound Sort Hoops"

Three hoops: /k/ | /e/ | other. Sort picture cards (cat, egg, sun, cup, bed). Say sound at start of each word.

Wrap-up (5min)

Air-write c and e while chanting sounds.

Teacher Tips

  • c and k make the same sound at start — teach as one sound family.
  • Short /e/ only; long vowels come in K3.

K2 Lesson 04: CVC Blending Blitz

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Blend 8+ CVC words in one session.
  • Read CVC words on flashcards without pictures.

Vocab

  • pen, hen, red, bed, pet, net, sit, pin

Warm-up (5min)

60-second blend drill — teacher flips cards, class blends aloud together.

Main Activity (20min): "Blending Blitz"

Timed rounds: Round 1 with pictures, Round 2 words only, Round 3 student picks a card and blends solo. Track team score on board.

Wrap-up (5min)

Take-home: 5 CVC flashcards to show parents.

Teacher Tips

  • Celebrate speed of sound-blending, not reading speed.
  • Swap weak pairs — some kids need picture support longer.

K2 Lesson 05: Dolch Primer: Structural Anchors

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Recognize: the, a, I, you, it, is.
  • Point to words in a shared sentence frame.

Vocab

  • the / a / I: "I see a cat." "It is the mat."

Warm-up (5min)

Word hunt on the morning message board — circle "the" and "a" with dry-erase markers.

Main Activity (20min): "Sentence Frame Build"

Pocket chart: I + see + a + [picture]. Students swap picture cards and read full sentence chorally, then solo.

Wrap-up (5min)

Copy one sentence with word cards (not handwriting yet).

Teacher Tips

  • Dolch Primer structural words unlock 50% of K2 readers.
  • Same 6 words daily for 2 weeks minimum.

K2 Lesson 06: Dolch Primer: Action Links

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Recognize: see, go, can, run, play, like.
  • Read 3-word phrases: "I can run." "I like cats."

Vocab

  • see / go / can: Action-linked sight words.
  • like: Thumbs up + "I like ___."

Warm-up (5min)

TPR sight word drill: teacher shows "run" — students run in place.

Main Activity (20min): "Action Link Cards"

Match sight word to action photo. Build phrases on the board: "I can ___." Students fill with play, run, go.

Wrap-up (5min)

Read 3 phrase cards to teacher at the door for exit.

Teacher Tips

  • Link every new sight word to a body action on day one.
  • Combine with Lesson 05 words in one sentence by week end.

K2 Lesson 07: Guided Reading: ORT Stage 2

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Follow a Level 2 ORT book with teacher guidance.
  • Answer "Who?" and "What?" after each page.

Vocab

  • Title / page / character: Book orientation language.
  • Story-specific words from chosen ORT Stage 2 title.

Warm-up (5min)

Cover preview: "What do you see? Who is this?" Predict the story.

Main Activity (25min): "Guided Read (Small Groups)"

Groups of 4 with teacher. Echo read → choral read → student points and reads one word. Rotate groups. Use finger tracking.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw favorite scene — tell one sentence about it.

Teacher Tips

  • ORT Stage 2 = 1 sentence per page; perfect for K2 entry.
  • Never send home unreadable books — confidence first.

K2 Lesson 08: Numbers & Quantities

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Count and label quantities 1-15.
  • Ask and answer "How many?" in a full sentence.

Vocab

  • How many?: "How many frogs? Three frogs."
  • There are ___: "There are five books."

Warm-up (5min)

Count classroom objects to 15 — touch each item while counting.

Main Activity (20min): "Quantity Match"

Numeral card + object pile matching. Partner A asks "How many?" Partner B answers in full sentence. Swap roles.

Wrap-up (5min)

Worksheet: draw 12 apples, cross out 4, answer "How many now?"

Teacher Tips

  • K2 moves from counting to quantity language — insist on full answers.
  • Use frogs, blocks, or bears — countable nouns only.

K2 Lesson 09: Colors & Descriptions

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Describe objects with color + noun: "a red ball."
  • Use big / small with color phrases.

Vocab

  • a red ___ / a big ___: Adjective before noun order.
  • What color is it?: "It is blue."

Warm-up (5min)

Color stand-up: teacher says "blue" — students wearing blue stand.

Main Activity (20min): "Description Bag"

Pull object from bag. Student says "It is a big red ___." Class repeats. Add second adjective when ready.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw and label: "a small green frog."

Teacher Tips

  • English adjective order (color before noun) is new — drill the pattern.
  • Contrast big/small with same color to isolate size.

K2 Lesson 10: Animals & Habitats

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Name 8 animals and their habitats (farm, sea, jungle).
  • Say "A ___ lives in the ___."

Vocab

  • fish / sea: "A fish lives in the sea."
  • cow / farm | tiger / jungle

Warm-up (5min)

Animal sound quiz — sound only, students guess animal.

Main Activity (20min): "Habitat Sort"

Three poster zones: Farm, Sea, Jungle. Sort animal cards. Each student places one card and says the full sentence.

Wrap-up (5min)

Favorite animal share: "I like ___. It lives in the ___."

Teacher Tips

  • Full sentence frame scaffolds grammar early.
  • Use photos, not cartoons, for habitat accuracy.

K2 Lesson 11: Family & Possessives

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Use my, your, his, her with family words.
  • Ask "Who is this?" and answer "This is my ___."

Vocab

  • my / your: "This is my mom."
  • his / her: Point to classmate's picture card.

Warm-up (5min)

Family finger puppets — introduce each with "This is my ___."

Main Activity (20min): "Possessive Picture Swap"

Students hold family cards. Partner asks "Who is this?" Answer with my/your. Switch cards — now use his/her for the partner's card.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw family — one label: "my dad" or "my sister."

Teacher Tips

  • Possessives are hard — use pointing + card swap to make abstract concrete.
  • Don't introduce his/her until my/your is solid.

K2 Lesson 12: Food Likes & Dislikes

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Say "I like ___." and "I don't like ___."
  • Ask "Do you like ___?" and answer Yes/No.

Vocab

  • like / don't like: Thumbs up / down.
  • pizza, rice, apple, fish, milk

Warm-up (5min)

Food flashcard quick poll — class stands if they like it.

Main Activity (20min): "Taste Chart Survey"

Students survey 3 friends: "Do you like apples?" Record yes/no on chart. Report: "Minho likes apples. Sara doesn't like fish."

Wrap-up (5min)

Sticker on personal "My Likes" plate drawing.

Teacher Tips

  • All food answers are valid — never tease dislikes.
  • Survey format teaches question form naturally.

K2 Lesson 13: Weather Week Chart

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Describe daily weather and temperature feel (hot, cold).
  • Record weather on a 5-day class chart.

Vocab

  • sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy
  • hot / cold: "It is hot today."

Warm-up (5min)

Window check + weather song. Student weather reporter of the day.

Main Activity (20min): "Week Chart Build"

Add today's weather icon to chart. Compare: "Monday was sunny. Today is rainy." Role-play dressing for weather (jacket, hat).

Wrap-up (5min)

Predict tomorrow's weather — any guess is fine.

Teacher Tips

  • Run this chart all year — one minute daily after Lesson 13.
  • Link hot/cold to what they wear — Korean kids connect quickly.

K2 Lesson 14: Action Verbs & Sentences

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Build SVO sentences: "I eat apples." "She runs fast."
  • Use 6 verbs: eat, run, jump, read, swim, sleep.

Vocab

  • I / He / She + verb: Subject-verb agreement intro (runs vs run simplified).

Warm-up (5min)

Verb charades — act eat, sleep, run; class guesses verb.

Main Activity (20min): "Sentence Builder"

Three pocket chart rows: Subject | Verb | Object. Teams build silly sentences: "She eats pizza." Read aloud with expression.

Wrap-up (5min)

Copy one sentence with word tiles.

Teacher Tips

  • First real grammar frame — keep verbs in present only.
  • Silly sentences increase retention ("I swim pizza" → laugh → fix).

K2 Lesson 15: Prepositions: In, On, Under

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Use in, on, under with classroom objects.
  • Follow "Put the book ___ the box."

Vocab

  • in / on / under: TPR with box and toy.
  • Where is it?: "It is on the table."

Warm-up (5min)

Teacher hides teddy — students ask "Where is it?" Hot/cold optional.

Main Activity (20min): "Preposition Obstacle"

Commands: "Jump on the mat! Crawl under the table! Put the ball in the box!" Pairs give one command each.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw teddy in, on, under a box — three panels.

Teacher Tips

  • Physical prepositions stick — always move the body.
  • Add behind/next to only after these three are automatic.

K2 Lesson 16: Story Time Read-Aloud

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Listen to a full picture book and sequence 3 events.
  • Retell beginning, middle, end with prompts.

Vocab

  • first / next / last: Sequencing language.
  • Story vocabulary from chosen ORT Stage 2-3 book.

Warm-up (5min)

Cover + title predict. "What might happen?"

Main Activity (25min): "Interactive Read-Aloud"

Teacher reads with stops: "What do you see?" "How does he feel?" After reading, sequence 3 picture cards as a class.

Wrap-up (5min)

Vote: favorite character and one-word reason.

Teacher Tips

  • Stop 3-4 times max — don't quiz every page.
  • Same book twice in two weeks deepens retell quality.

K2 Lesson 17: Phonics Review Game

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Review Groups 1-3 sounds and CVC blending.
  • Apply phonics in a low-pressure game format.

Vocab

  • All Group 1-3 letter sounds + 10 CVC review words.

Warm-up (5min)

Phonics action relay — full alphabet actions learned so far.

Main Activity (25min): "Phonics Board Game"

Board with spaces: "Say the sound" / "Blend this word" / "Act the letter." Teams roll dice, complete task, advance. No elimination.

Wrap-up (5min)

Whole class blends 5 cards — fastest friendly cheer.

Teacher Tips

  • End-of-unit game — keep energy high, correction gentle.
  • Pair strong blenders with hesitant peers as helpers.

K2 Lesson 18: K2 Final Assessment

Age: 4-5 years

Duration: 40 min

Objectives

  • Demonstrate CVC blending, 10 sight words, and one full sentence.
  • Complete play-based stations (no formal written test).

Vocab

  • Review: CVC list, Dolch primer set, "I like / I can / It is" frames.

Warm-up (5min)

"K2 Ready" chant — phonics actions + hello song.

Main Activity (25min): "Assessment Stations"

4 stations (6 min rotate): (1) Blend 5 CVC cards (2) Sight word slap (3) Read ORT page with teacher (4) Say one full sentence about family or food. Checklist only — no grades shown to students.

Wrap-up (5min)

K2 certificate + "Next: K3 Pre-Starters!" preview.

Teacher Tips

  • Observation rubric only — never post scores in class.
  • Use results to place K3 groups (blend-ready vs sight-word focus).
