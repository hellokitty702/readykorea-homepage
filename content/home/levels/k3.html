K3 — Ages 5-6

Core Competency: Full sentence construction, Phonics 4-5, Pre-Starters readiness.
18-unit curriculum for Pre-Starters Mastery.

K3 Lesson 01: Advanced Quantities

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Count and describe quantities with subject + verb: "Three frogs jump."
  • Use many / few / some in simple phrases.

Vocab

  • three frogs / jumps: "Three frogs jump!"
  • many / few: "Many birds. Few cats."

Warm-up (5min)

Counting chant 1-20 with jumping — one jump per number.

Main Activity (25min): "Active Counting Loop"

Teacher sets quantity cards (3, 5, 8). Students perform action that many times: "Five claps!" "Eight hops!" Build to full sentence on board.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw quantity scene — label with one full sentence.

Teacher Tips

  • K3 requires full sentences — no one-word answers.
  • Combine number + noun + verb every time.

K3 Lesson 02: Full Sentence Frames (I have...)

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Produce "I have ___." and "I don't have ___." reliably.
  • Ask "Do you have ___?" with Yes/No answers.

Vocab

  • I have / I don't have: School bag items.
  • Do you have?: Partner interview frame.

Warm-up (5min)

Show-your-bag: hold up pencil, eraser — "I have a pencil."

Main Activity (25min): "Have/Don't Have Survey"

Checklist of 6 items. Interview 4 friends. Report: "Jin has a ruler. Mina doesn't have scissors."

Wrap-up (5min)

Write one have + one don't have sentence with word tiles.

Teacher Tips

  • Negative form is new — model "don't" as one chunk.
  • Use real bag items for authenticity.

K3 Lesson 03: Long Vowel Magic: Silent E

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Understand silent e changes short to long vowel (cap → cape).
  • Read 6 magic-e words: cake, bike, rope, cube, kite, home.

Vocab

  • Magic e: "The e is quiet — it makes the vowel say its name."

Warm-up (5min)

Compare pairs: cap/cape, kit/kite — clap short vs long vowel.

Main Activity (25min): "Magic E Wand"

Add paper "e" wand to CVC cards. Read new word aloud. Sort short vs long vowel piles.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw magic-e picture (kite) and label.

Teacher Tips

  • Don't teach rule abstractly — always show cap→cape transform.
  • Limit to 6 words this lesson.

K3 Lesson 04: Phonics Groups 4-5 Intro

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Learn Jolly Phonics Group 4-5 sounds with actions.
  • Blend CVCC and CCVC patterns (stop, frog, clap).

Vocab

  • Group 4-5 letter actions + stop / frog / clap / grab

Warm-up (5min)

Review Groups 1-3 actions in 60-second drill.

Main Activity (25min): "Blend Ladder"

Climb ladder board: each rung adds a sound — s-t-o-p. Teams blend before advancing.

Wrap-up (5min)

Group 4-5 song chorus.

Teacher Tips

  • Groups 4-5 unlock Pre-Starters reading band.
  • Consonant clusters need slow stretch blending.

K3 Lesson 05: ORT Magic Key Prep

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Preview Magic Key series characters and story structure.
  • Read ORT Stage 4-5 sample pages with teacher support.

Vocab

  • key / magic / adventure: ORT Magic Key theme words.

Warm-up (5min)

Character poster tour — Biff, Chip, Kipper, Floppy.

Main Activity (30min): "Magic Key First Read"

Small groups read 4 pages. Stop after each: "What happened?" Predict next page.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw the magic key — "Where would you go?"

Teacher Tips

  • Magic Key is the K3 milestone — celebrate it visibly.
  • Multi-sentence pages OK with choral support.

K3 Lesson 06: Numbers 11-20 & Compare

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Count 11-20 fluently.
  • Use more than / less than with numbers.

Vocab

  • eleven – twenty
  • more than / less than: "12 is more than 10."

Warm-up (5min)

Number line hop 11-20 on floor tape.

Main Activity (25min): "Compare Cards"

Two number cards — student says comparison sentence. Partner verifies with objects.

Wrap-up (5min)

Worksheet: circle the bigger number, write one sentence.

Teacher Tips

  • Teens are tricky — chant 11-20 daily for 2 weeks.
  • Compare only after counting is solid.

K3 Lesson 07: Classroom Rules & Routines

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • State 5 classroom rules in full sentences.
  • Explain why with "because": "We listen because..."

Vocab

  • raise your hand / take turns / be kind
  • because: First causal connector.

Warm-up (5min)

Rule charades — act raise hand, share, walk quietly.

Main Activity (25min): "Rule Poster Teams"

Each team makes one rule poster with picture + sentence + because clause. Present to class.

Wrap-up (5min)

Class pledge: recite all 5 rules together.

Teacher Tips

  • Co-create rules — student ownership increases compliance.
  • "Because" preview feeds Lesson 12.

K3 Lesson 08: Animals & Adjectives

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Describe animals with 2 adjectives: "A big brown bear."
  • Compare two animals: "The elephant is bigger."

Vocab

  • big / small / fast / slow / furry / scary

Warm-up (5min)

Animal sound + adjective: "A loud lion!" "A quiet mouse!"

Main Activity (25min): "Adjective Zoo"

Stations with animal photos. Each student writes/says two-adjective phrase. Compare pairs at elephant vs mouse station.

Wrap-up (5min)

Favorite animal describe: "I like ___ because it is ___."

Teacher Tips

  • Adjective order: size before color before noun.
  • Comparative -er only (bigger) — not "more big."

K3 Lesson 09: Family & Descriptions

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Describe family members with age and appearance.
  • Say "My ___ is ___ years old." and "She/He is tall."

Vocab

  • years old / tall / short / young / old

Warm-up (5min)

Family photo share — one sentence per student.

Main Activity (25min): "Family Book Page"

Each student makes one page: drawing + 3 sentences (name, age, look). Bind into class book.

Wrap-up (5min)

Read partner's page aloud.

Teacher Tips

  • Introduce he/she consistently for family members.
  • Keep age numbers 1-99 only.

K3 Lesson 10: Food & Drinks Order

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Order food politely: "Can I have ___ please?"
  • Role-play cafe server and customer.

Vocab

  • Can I have / please / thank you
  • juice, water, sandwich, cookie, milk

Warm-up (5min)

Menu chant — picture menu on board.

Main Activity (25min): "Class Cafe"

Set up pretend cafe. Server asks "What do you want?" Customer: "Can I have juice, please?" Rotate roles.

Wrap-up (5min)

Write order on order slip — read back to teacher.

Teacher Tips

  • Politeness frames are high-value for Korean parents.
  • Plastic food + tray = instant engagement.

K3 Lesson 11: Weather & Clothes

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Match weather to clothing: "It is rainy. I wear a coat."
  • Name 8 clothing items.

Vocab

  • coat, boots, hat, T-shirt, shorts, scarf, gloves, umbrella

Warm-up (5min)

Weather report + dress-the-paper-doll on board.

Main Activity (25min): "Weather Wardrobe"

Draw weather card — pick clothes from pile. Say full sentence. Partner checks match.

Wrap-up (5min)

Tomorrow's outfit predict + sentence.

Teacher Tips

  • Link to real window weather every class.
  • "I wear" not "I put" — model correct verb.

K3 Lesson 12: Feelings & Because

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Express feelings with reason: "I am happy because..."
  • Use sad, angry, tired, excited, scared, proud.

Vocab

  • I am ___ because ___: Sentence frame on board all lesson.

Warm-up (5min)

Feelings flash + because prompt: "I am tired because I ran."

Main Activity (25min): "Because Chain"

Circle share: each adds feeling + because. Next student repeats previous + adds own.

Wrap-up (5min)

Exit ticket sentence on sticky note.

Teacher Tips

  • Because-clause is K3 grammar milestone.
  • Accept any logical reason — don't correct content.

K3 Lesson 13: Shapes & Patterns

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Name shapes + patterns: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star.
  • Continue AB and ABB patterns (red-blue-red-blue).

Vocab

  • pattern / next / repeat

Warm-up (5min)

Clap-stomp AB pattern — students continue.

Main Activity (25min): "Pattern Makers"

Beads or blocks — build pattern, partner extends. Describe: "My pattern is red, blue, red, blue."

Wrap-up (5min)

Shape pattern worksheet — color next 3 items.

Teacher Tips

  • Patterns bridge math + English — use manipulatives.
  • Verbal describe pattern, not just continue silently.

K3 Lesson 14: Sight Words Advanced

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Read 15 Dolch Primer words in isolation and in sentences.
  • Build 5-word sentences from word bank.

Vocab

  • Review K2 set + where, come, said, here, there, this, that, they

Warm-up (5min)

Sight word speed drill — 30 seconds per row.

Main Activity (25min): "Sentence Factory"

Word bank tiles — build longest correct sentence. Read to class. Swap one word — still correct?

Wrap-up (5min)

Take-home flash ring — 5 new words highlighted.

Teacher Tips

  • 15 words max new per lesson — spiral rest daily.
  • Sentence factory beats copying drills.

K3 Lesson 15: Story Comprehension

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Answer Who, What, Where after a story.
  • Retell 3 events in order with first / next / last.

Vocab

  • Who / What / Where / When question words on wall.

Warm-up (5min)

Question word song — point to wall chart.

Main Activity (30min): "Read & Quiz"

ORT Stage 5 read-aloud. Stop 4 times for Who/What/Where. Partner retell with picture sequence cards.

Wrap-up (5min)

Draw 3-panel retell strip.

Teacher Tips

  • Comprehension > decoding speed at K3.
  • Allow L1 hint for Who if name is hard — then repeat in English.

K3 Lesson 16: Pre-Starters Listening Drill

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Follow YLE Pre-Starters listening task formats (color, draw line, tick box).
  • Listen for specific nouns and colors in short dialogues.

Vocab

  • Exam task language: color / draw / look at / listen

Warm-up (5min)

"Listen carefully" ritual — ears ready pose.

Main Activity (30min): "Mock Listening Parts 1-2"

Teacher reads script twice. Students color correct picture, draw line to match. Use official YLE task layout on worksheet.

Wrap-up (5min)

Self-check with answer key — no grades, just ticks.

Teacher Tips

  • Always play/read twice — train exam habit early.
  • Keep anxiety low: "practice game" framing.

K3 Lesson 17: Pre-Starters Speaking Drill

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Answer personal questions: name, age, family, favorites.
  • Describe picture with 3+ sentences (exam-style).

Vocab

  • Question set: What's your name? How old are you? What's this?

Warm-up (5min)

Ball toss question game — catch, answer, throw.

Main Activity (30min): "Examiner Rotation"

Teacher or advanced student as examiner. 2-minute mock per student: personal Q + picture describe. Rotate through class in pairs while others do picture prep.

Wrap-up (5min)

One thing I did well today — thumb vote.

Teacher Tips

  • Full sentences rewarded even if grammar imperfect.
  • Picture describe: teach There is / They are frames first.

K3 Lesson 18: K3 Final Exam Prep

Age: 5-6 years

Duration: 45 min

Objectives

  • Review phonics 4-5, sight words, sentence frames, and YLE task types.
  • Complete calm mock stations — readiness check for G1/Starters track.

Vocab

  • Full K3 review mix — no new vocabulary introduced.

Warm-up (5min)

Phonics action medley Groups 1-5.

Main Activity (35min): "Graduation Stations"

5 stations: (1) Blend & read (2) Sight sentence build (3) Listen & color (4) Picture speak (5) ORT page read. 6 min each, checklist for teacher only.

Wrap-up (5min)

K3 certificate + "Welcome G1 Starters path!" celebration.

Teacher Tips

  • No scores posted — use data for G1 placement only.
  • End on celebration, not correction.
