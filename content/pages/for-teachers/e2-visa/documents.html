Essential packing

CLOTHING & SIZES

Larger Western sizes (US men\'s 11+ shoes, tall fits) are hard to find in Korea — bring sufficient everyday clothes and shoes.

PERSONAL GROOMING

Deodorant is especially hard to find locally. Bring 6+ months\' supply.

ELECTRONICS

Unlocked smartphone for Korean SIM/eSIM on Day 1. Plug adapters (Type C/F, 220V) × 3–5.
