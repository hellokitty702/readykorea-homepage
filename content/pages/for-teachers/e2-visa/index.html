E-2 strategy at a glance

  1. Sign contract — start FBI check + degree apostille immediately (plan for 8–12 weeks in 2026, not 4–6)
  2. School applies for VIN — Korean Immigration issues Visa Issuance Number (1–3 weeks after docs arrive)
  3. Consulate stamping — book via 365 Portal; visa in hand before you book flights
  4. Land in Korea — MOJ medical exam + ARC within 90 days; cannot teach at a new workplace until immigration confirms
