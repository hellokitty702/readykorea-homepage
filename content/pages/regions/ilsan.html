Overview

Ilsan (일산) is the western anchor of Goyang city — a 1990s–2000s new town built around Ilsan Lake Park, now one of Gyeonggi\'s most livable zones for family hagwon teachers. Line 3 runs north–south through the residential core; Gyeongui-Jungang links you to Hongdae, Yongsan, and central Seoul without crossing Gangnam.

Best for: teachers who want parks, planned sidewalks, and Hongdae access on one train line. Tradeoff: fewer listings than Daechi or Bundang — and Jeongbalsan vs Daehwa can be 25 min apart by bus.

    Culture & vibe

    🍜 Local eats

    • Jeongbalsan restaurant row — ₩8–12K lunch sets, cafe density
    • Lafesta / Western Dom — mall food courts, movie nights
    • Lake Park picnic culture — chicken delivery to the grass — teacher tradition
    • Paju Heyri day trip — art village 30 min north, weekend default

    📚 Study spots

    • Starbucks Jeongbalsan — reliable WiFi, teacher work-coffee hub
    • Lake Park benches — outdoor grading spring/fall (bring mat)
    • Goyang Central Library — quiet floors near Lake Park
    • 24-hour study cafes — ₩5–7K standard Gyeonggi pricing

    🌃 Nightlife

    • Hongdae via Gyeongui-Jungang — ~30 min from Daehwa, monthly batch trip
    • Jeongbalsan noraebang — local staff bonding, not tourist zone
    • Lafesta cinema + bars — calmer than Seoul, mall-cluster evenings
    • Lake Park night lights — seasonal festivals, free date spot

    Expat tips

    1. "Ilsan" spans two subway lines. Jeongbalsan (Line 3) to Daehwa (Gyeongui) is 20+ min — match housing to your hagwon pin on Naver.
    2. Gyeongui-Jungang is your Hongdae lifeline. Daehwa→Hongdae ~30 min off-peak — viable for monthly nightlife, not daily if you teach east Ilsan.
    3. Lake Park is free therapy. 91 hectares — run, bike, picnic after hagwon. Best perk of west Gyeonggi placement.
    4. Costco Paju is closer than Seoul. Split warehouse runs with roommates — west Gyeonggi teachers batch imports quarterly.
    5. English thinner than Bundang. Papago for clinics and banks — Jeongbalsan commercial zone has more English signage than KINTEX west fringe.
