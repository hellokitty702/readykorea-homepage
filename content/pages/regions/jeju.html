Overview

Jeju (제주) is Korea\'s only major island teaching market — mostly EPIK public schools and a smaller hagwon layer in Jeju City and Seogwipo. Teachers come for Hallasan hikes, Aewol cafe coast sunsets, and a tight expat WhatsApp network. The tradeoff: fewer jobs, flight costs to mainland, and you really want a car (or a hagwon director who provides shuttle).

Best for: nature-first teachers, EPIK applicants, slow-lane Korea. Tradeoff: lowest hagwon density in our guides — verify contract location before signing.

    Culture & vibe

    🍜 Local eats

    • Black pork (흑돼지) — Jeju specialty BBQ, payday ritual
    • Haemul-tang — seafood stew, Seogwipo classic
    • Aewol cafe coast — sunset americano runs every weekend
    • Jeju City night market — street food, local omegi rice cakes

    📚 Study spots

    • Aewol coastal cafes — laptop-friendly with ocean view
    • Starbucks Jeju downtown — reliable WiFi when cafes close
    • Jeju Central Library — quiet floors, AC in summer
    • Hagwon break room — lesson prep reality on island placements

    🌃 Nightlife

    • Jeju City downtown bars — small expat + local mix
    • Seogwipo waterfront — calmer evening walks
    • Noraebang Jeju City — staff bonding after payday
    • Monthly mainland flight — Seoul/Busan when island feels small

    Expat tips

    1. Car or no car — decide before signing. Many EPIK schools rural; hagwon Jeju City jobs walkable. Naver-pin your apartment to school.
    2. Join Jeju expat groups immediately. Smaller pool — car shares, flight deals, and clinic recommendations circulate on Kakao.
    3. Budget mainland flights. ₩100K+ round-trip to Seoul — factor into annual savings vs peninsula placements.
    4. Typhoon season Aug–Sep. Flights cancel — keep 1–2 days buffer for visa runs or holidays.
    5. English services thinner than Seoul. Jeju National Univ Hospital for serious care; Papago for daily clinics.
