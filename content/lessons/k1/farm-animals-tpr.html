K1 Lesson 06: Farm Animals TPR

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Cow · Pig · Duck · Sheep · Horse · Chicken · Animal sounds · /m/ moo · /d/ duck

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Animal picture cards (6): cow · pig · duck · sheep · horse · chicken — large photo + word
  • Animal headbands (6 sets): one per animal — enough for class rotation during parade
  • Sound prompt cards (6): moo · oink · quack · baa · neigh · cluck — icon + sound text
  • Old MacDonald audio: speaker or phone (chorus clip, 30–45 sec)
  • Phonics flashcards: cow picture + /m/ moo · duck picture + /d/ duck
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Hello / Goodbye cards: brief greeting from Lesson 02
  • Parade path markers: tape line or carpet edge for Animal Parade route
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Optional: barn basket for headband storage between rounds

Learning Objectives

  • Students name six farm animals: cow, pig, duck, sheep, horse, chicken.
  • Students mimic animal sounds (moo, oink, quack, baa, neigh, cluck) with matching gestures.
  • Students participate in Animal Parade with headbands — "I am a duck! Quack quack!"
  • Students respond to "The cow says ___" imitation prompts with sound or word.
  • Students hear and respond to /m/ (moo) and /d/ (duck) with animal picture cards and full animal TPR.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Channel farm-animal excitement into safe circle routines, then build sound-and-gesture fluency before the Animal Parade.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, preview today's farm animal theme.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, friends! Hello! Welcome to the farm! Sit on your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "We are in the same safe circle. Today we meet farm animals!"
"Cow, pig, duck, sheep, horse, chicken. We moo, we oink, we quack! Are you ready?"

Teacher · Animal card peek

"Peek — cow!" (flash cow card) "Peek — duck!" (flash duck card)
"Animals visit our circle today — but first — quiet barn. Look — Listen — Sit."
"Save your moo for later — listen first!"

Teacher · Parade path preview

"See the tape line? That is our farm path. Walking feet — one lap — then back to your spot."
"When you wear a headband — you ARE that animal. Gentle hands — headbands stay on your head."
"Parade comes after sounds — listen first, farm friends!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child makes loud animal sounds during Meeting Up, hold Listen card: "Save your moo for later. Listen first — then big moo!"

If a child says "Dog!" instead of farm animals, redirect: "Dogs are friends too! Today — farm — cow! Moo!"

If a child grabs a headband early, gentle hand: "Headbands wait in the barn basket. Sit on your spot first."

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — hold up three fingers, then point to your eyes. Wait for whole-group attention before showing animal cards.
Management Cue: If animal sounds get too loud during Meeting Up, hold Listen card — channel excitement, don't shut it down.
Management Cue: Keep headbands out of reach until Parade — visible but not touchable during Meeting Up.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit with cards — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Flash six animal cards quickly — "Today these animals visit our circle!"
  4. Name each animal once with picture — cow, pig, duck, sheep, horse, chicken (≈1 min).
  5. Preview parade path with finger — "We walk this line later — walking feet!"
  6. Quick hello review from Lesson 02 — wave and say Hello to the person beside you (≈30 sec).
  7. Feeling check: "How are you on the farm? I'm happy!" — thumbs up before animal sounds (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + review link to prior lessons plus Old MacDonald sound chorus.

Teacher · Greeting + command review

"Hello, friends! How are you? I'm happy! Thumbs up!"
"Magic words — Look! Listen! Sit! You remember every week — wonderful!"

Teacher · Old MacDonald sound warm-up

"Old MacDonald had a farm — E-I-E-I-O! On his farm he had a cow — E-I-E-I-O!"
"With a moo moo here and a moo moo there! Everyone — MOO!" (hands like cow horns)
"Pig — OINK! Duck — QUACK! Sheep — BAA! Your turn — copy my animal!"

Teacher · Animal sound chain

"Sound chain! I say an animal — you say the sound. Cow!"
(Class) "Moo!"
"Pig!" (Class) "Oink!" "Duck!" (Class) "Quack!"
"Horse — neigh! Chicken — cluck cluck! You are a noisy farm!"

Teacher · Animal name echo

"Echo the animal names — cow! pig! duck! sheep! horse! chicken!"
"Mix order — duck! cow! pig! You know farm animals!"
"Stand up in 3, 2, 1 — Animal Parade time — walking feet!"
Handling Mistakes:

If one child dominates with loud sounds, assign turn-taking: "This side moos — that side oinks." Split volume across the circle.

If a child makes a nonsense sound ("Bzz!"), redirect: "Fun bug! Our farm sound — moo! Copy cow horns — moo!"

If children stand during sound warm-up, point to Sit card: "Sit — sound — gesture. All three together."

Management Cue: Model gesture + sound together every time — cow horns + moo, duck bill hands + quack. Never sound-only at K1.
Management Cue: Split the group if volume is too high: "Girls moo — boys oink!" then switch — keeps everyone participating without chaos.
Management Cue: After Old MacDonald chorus, use Listen card + 3-second wait before Parade setup — energy transition is critical.
  1. Hello + How are you? — whole group (≈1 min).
  2. Look · Listen · Sit drill — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  3. Old MacDonald chorus: cow, pig, duck, sheep — sound + gesture × 1 pass each (≈2 min).
  4. Animal sound chain: teacher names animal, class responds with sound × full set of six (≈1 min).
  5. Quick review: hold two animal cards — "Which says moo? Which says quack?" — point only (≈30 sec).
  6. Transition: "You know the sounds — now wear the animals! Animal Parade!"

Main Activity: Animal Parade & /m/ /d/ Phonics (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Sound + motion together — K1 learns animals through the body first. Accept animal sounds as valid answers before clear word production. Return to circle for phonics and Calming Down.

Review: Use hello/goodbye from Lesson 02 and Look · Listen · Sit from Lesson 01 at every transition during this lesson.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — Animal Parade with headbands: line up, parade, introduce self as animal.

Teacher · Parade setup

"Animal Parade! You wear a headband — you ARE that animal. Walk slow — walking feet — around our farm path."
"When I stop you, say: I am a duck! Quack quack!" (model with duck headband)
"Listen — gentle parade. No running. Hands hold your headband — don't pull a friend's."

Teacher · Parade rounds

"Round one — half the class parades. Cow says moo! Pig says oink! Stop — introduce yourself!"
"Round two — swap headbands! New animal — new sound! Horse — neigh! Chicken — cluck cluck!"
"Round three — whole farm together! Line up — parade — all animals at once. Moo! Oink! Quack! Baa!"

Teacher · "The cow says ___" prompt game

"Listen — The cow says… moo! The pig says… oink!"
"Your turn — The duck says… (wait) quack quack! Wonderful!"
"The sheep says… baa! The horse says… neigh! The chicken says… cluck cluck!"
"I am a [animal]! [sound]! — say it when you stop in the parade!"

Teacher · Parade stop introductions

"Stop — freeze — introduce! [Name] — who are you?"
(Child) "I am a pig! Oink oink!"
"Class — copy — I am a pig! Oink! Wonderful parade voice!"
"Next friend — stop — who are you? I am a sheep! Baa baa!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child says "Cat!" for The cow says, smile: "Cats say meow — cows say moo! The cow says moo!"

If parade introduction is only a sound (no "I am a…"), accept then extend: "Great moo! Now full sentence — I am a cow! Moo!"

If headbands slip over eyes, fix calmly: "Headband on forehead — like a crown. Can you see? Good — parade!"

If a child refuses to swap headbands in Round 2, offer choice: "Keep cow or try duck — you pick. Both are brave farm animals."

Management Cue: Parade path before Round 1: define the route with tape — "Walk this line only. Return to your spot when you finish one lap." Prevents wandering.
Management Cue: Headband rules: "Headbands stay on your head — not a weapon, not a toy." Collect and inspect headbands between rounds for bent bands.
Management Cue: Half-class parades only — seated children watch and make the sound when parade stops — keeps all engaged.
  1. Teacher models parade with one headband — full lap + "I am a duck! Quack!" (≈1 min).
  2. Round 1: half class parades with cow/pig/duck headbands (≈2 min).
  3. Round 2: swap headbands — sheep/horse/chicken (≈2 min).
  4. Round 3: optional fast group parade — all sounds chorus (≈2 min).
  5. Seated children echo each parade stop: "I am a cow! Moo!" — builds audience participation (≈30 sec).
  6. Headbands off, collected in basket — "Parade finished! Sit on your spot."

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Phonics /m/ moo (cow) and /d/ duck — animal picture cards + full animal TPR + peer cheer.

Teacher · Introduce /m/ moo

"Cow says MOO! Moo starts with M! M says m-m-moo. Mmm-mmm-mmm. Lips together — hum. Mmm!"
"This is cow." (show cow card) "Cow TPR — hands on head like horns — moo! Mmm — moo!"
"Stand up! Cow horns — moo! Sit down — quiet mmm."

Teacher · Introduce /d/ duck + partner cheer

"Duck says QUACK — and duck starts with D! D says d-d-duck. Duh-duh-duh. Tongue tap — duh!"
"This is duck." (show duck card) "Duck TPR — arms like wings — flap flap — quack quack! Duh — duck!"
"Partner cheer: you are cow — mmm moo! Friend is duck — flap quack! Switch!"

Teacher · Full-body animal TPR check

"Cow card — horns — mmm moo! Duck card — wings — duh quack!"
"Stand up — cow! Sit down. Stand up — duck! Sit down."
"Last check — hold both cards — moo or quack? Listen for the picture!"

Teacher · Praise frames

"Wow! I heard mmm for moo!"
"Super duck sound — quack quack!"
"Your horns and wings are perfect — cow and duck!"
"High five for listening — moo and quack!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child confuses moo and quack during phonics, show cards side by side: "Cow — mmm — horns. Duck — duh — wings."

If TPR becomes running around, reset: "Sit — horns — moo. Sit — wings — quack. In your spot."

If a child won't do partner cheer, teacher pairs with child: "You are my cow — moo! I am duck — quack!"

If /m/ has no voice (just breath), hand on throat: "Feel the buzz — mmm — moo!"

Management Cue: Before standing TPR, say "Stand up in 3, 2, 1" and "Sit down in 3, 2, 1" every time — same cue as Lessons 02–05.
Management Cue: Assign shoulder partners before cheer — "Turn to your neighbor" reduces running across the circle.
Management Cue: Accept moo/quack without clear /m/ or /d/ isolation — phonics is exposure at K1; praise effort loudly.
  1. Show cow card — model /m/ × 3, cow horn TPR × 3.
  2. Show duck card — model /d/ × 3, duck wing flap × 3.
  3. Alternating drill: teacher holds cow or duck card — class responds with sound + full animal TPR.
  4. Partner cheer: 30 seconds each side — cow moo / duck quack exchange.
  5. Whole-group check: cow card → mmm + horns; duck card → duh + wing flap.

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Put animals "to sleep" in the barn basket, lower energy, affirm parade participation, dismiss with Goodbye Song.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Purpose: Breathing, compliment, goodbye song, dismissal.

Teacher · Calm breathing

"Farm time is finished. Sit on your spot. Hands in lap — quiet barn."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… mmm, 1, 2, 3. Soft cow breath out."
"Again. In… 1, 2, 3. Out… duh… quiet duck breath."

Teacher · Compliment circle

"Today you were farm animals! You paraded with headbands. You mooed and quacked."
"I am proud of [name] for a brave I am a… sentence. I am proud of [name] for gentle parade feet."
"Everyone gets a sticker. Goodbye Song next — quiet barn."

Teacher · Goodbye Song + farm farewell

"Goodbye Song — wave! Goodbye, goodbye, see you again!"
"Goodbye cow — moo moo! Goodbye duck — quack quack!"
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye farm friends — see you again!"
"Line up at the door. High five — bye bye! Walking feet — quiet barn — animals sleep now."
Handling Mistakes:

If a child keeps whispering "moo" during breathing, accept softly: "Tiny moo is OK — softer — mmm — sleepy cow."

If a child tries to wear headband during Goodbye Song, redirect: "Animals sleep in the barn basket. Wave goodbye!"

If line-up becomes a stampede, pause song: "Walking feet — same line as yesterday — you can do it."

If a child is sad to remove headband, validate: "Duck goes in the barn to sleep. You can be duck again next time!"

Management Cue: Headbands in basket before breathing — "Animals sleep in the barn basket." Visual cleanup signal calms the group.
Management Cue: Dismiss in table order or color groups — never "everyone run to the door." Assign line leader weekly for predictable exit.
Management Cue: Compliment 2–3 children by name — rotate daily so every child hears their name within two weeks.
Management Cue: End with the same farm farewell verse every week — children predict the close and transition calmly to the door.
  1. Headbands away + two breath cycles (≈1 min).
  2. Compliments + stickers — name 2–3 specific parade moments (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye Song with farm farewell verse (≈2 min).
  4. Line up in table order — high-five exit ritual (≈1 min).
  5. Teacher waves at door: "Goodbye farm! See you next week!" — consistent exit cue for Lesson 07.
