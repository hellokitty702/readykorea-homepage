K1 Lesson 15: Phonics Group 1 — i, p, n

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes /i/ insect · /p/ puff · /n/ net · pin · nip · pan · Group 1 complete · s-a-t-i-p-n drill

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Letter cards (6): s · a · t · i · p · n — full Group 1 set
  • New letter cards (3): i · p · n — with Jolly action icons
  • Picture cue cards (3): insect · puff · net — photo + start sound
  • CVC word cards (3): pin · nip · pan — large print
  • Review CVC cards (3): sat · at · tap — spiral from Lesson 14
  • Blending rail (1): pocket strip for 3-letter slides
  • Group 1 station markers (6): s-a-t-i-p-n labels for relay layout
  • Group 1 song audio: full s-a-t-i-p-n run-through clip
  • Review hoop labels (3): s / a / t — spiral review from Lesson 14
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child — "Group 1 Complete" optional badge

Learning Objectives

  • Students hear and repeat /i/, /p/, /n/ with Jolly Phonics actions (insect, puff, net).
  • Students review s, a, t from Lesson 14 before adding new sounds.
  • Students blend CVC patterns with support: pin, nip, pan.
  • Students complete Phonics Group 1 (s-a-t-i-p-n) through a linked i+p+n drill connecting to Lesson 14.
  • Students run the full Group 1 action chain and celebrate finishing the first phonics set.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Spiral review s-a-t, preview i-p-n, energize with Group 1 action chain before CVC blending.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, phonics friends! Hello! Same safe circle — sit on your dot."
"Look · Listen · Sit. Last week — s, a, t. Today — three more sounds — i, p, n!"
"When we finish today — Group 1 is complete — six sounds together!"

Teacher · Spiral review peek — Lesson 14 link

"Quick peek — sss snake! A-a-a apple! T-t-t tap! You remember — wonderful!"
"Whisper blend from last week — sat… at… tap. Same blending today — new words!"
"New friends today — i insect! P puff! N net! Listen first — then move."

Teacher · Group 1 poster reveal

"Group 1 poster — s-a-t — you know these! Peel the sticky notes — i-p-n hiding underneath!"
"Six sounds on one train — s-a-t-i-p-n — chug chug chug! Almost full!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child cannot recall s-a-t, model one at a time without pressure: "Watch my snake — sss — we practice together."

If a child says "Group 2!" prematurely, smile: "Almost! Three more sounds today — then Group 1 party!"

Management Cue: Post Group 1 poster (s-a-t-i-p-n) visible but cover i-p-n with sticky notes until Step 2 reveal.
  1. Door greeting + circle settle with Look · Listen · Sit.
  2. Spiral flash: s, a, t with one action each (≈1 min).
  3. Whisper review blend: sat, at, tap (≈30 sec).
  4. Preview i, p, n cards face-down — "Three secrets today!"
  5. Hello wave to neighbor (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · s-a-t review chain

"Review train! Sss snake — a-a-a apple — t-t-t tap. Faster — s-a-t!"
"Last week's words — sat — at — tap — say them with me — sat, at, tap!"

Teacher · New sound — I insect

"New sound one — I — insect! I-i-i — itchy finger wiggle — i-i-i!"
"Watch — short i — not ee — tiny itch — i-i-i! Copy my wiggle!"
"Side A — i-i-i! Side B — i-i-i! Whole class — insect sound!"

Teacher · New sounds — P puff and N net

"P — puff! P-p-p — puff your cheeks — p-p-p! N — net! N-n-n — scoop arms like a net — n-n-n!"
"All new three — i-i-i, p-p-p, n-n-n! Say them slow… faster!"

Teacher · Six-sound preview chain

"All six — slow — sss, a-a-a, t-t-t, i-i-i, p-p-p, n-n-n! Group 1 train!"
"Stand up — six actions in a row — sit down. You are almost Group 1 masters!"

Teacher · First-sound check

"I hold a picture — insect — i-i-i or p-p-p? I-i-i! Puff picture — p-p-p! Net — n-n-n! Clever ears!"
Handling Mistakes:

If i-i-i becomes "ee," model short vowel: "Tiny insect itch — i-i-i — not ee."

If puff cheeks becomes raspberries, laugh briefly: "Funny lips! Soft puff — p-p-p."

If net motion hits a friend, reset: "Net scoops air — not friends. Arms gentle."

Management Cue: Add only ONE new sound per minute — do not rush all three i-p-n at once.
  1. s-a-t action review × 2 passes + whisper sat/at/tap (≈1 min).
  2. Introduce i, then p, then n — one action each × 2 (≈2 min).
  3. Six-sound slow chain — teacher leads (≈1 min).
  4. First-sound check: insect, puff, net pictures (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "Six sounds ready — now blend pin, nip, pan!"

Main Activity: CVC Blending & i+p+n Drill (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up blends pin, nip, pan; Cheering Up links i+p+n to Lesson 14's s+a+t in a full Group 1 drill. Spiral s-a-t inside every cheer round.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Teacher · pin blend

"Watch the rail — p… i… n. Slide close — p-i-n — pin! Pin!" (hold pin card + pinch fingers)
"Pin — like a sharp pin — careful hands — pin! Say it slow — p — i — n — pin!"

Teacher · nip and pan blends

"Same letters, new order — n… i… p — nip! Nip!" (gentle pinch motion)
"New start — p… a… n — pan! Pan!" (hold pan picture card)
"Round two — I point — you blend. p-i-n?" (wait) "Pin! n-i-p?" (wait) "Nip! p-a-n?" (wait) "Pan!"

Teacher · Link to Lesson 14 — sat and pin

"Last week — sat, at, tap. This week — pin, nip, pan. Same blending — slow… fast!"
"Sat and pin both have three sounds — listen to the ends — sat ends t-t-t, pin ends n-n-n!"
"Chart on the board — sat ←→ pin — same middle i-i-i sound in pin — different start!"

Teacher · Spiral blend mix

"Mix time — sat! Pin! Tap! Pan! I point — you blend — no pause — sat — pin — at — nip!"
"Wonderful — you blend old words AND new words — phonics stars!"

Teacher · Choral praise frames

"I heard pin! Super blending! Your mouth said every sound — p-i-n — pin!"
"If it's hard — watch my rail — I slide slow — you copy — pan!"
Handling Mistakes:

If pin becomes "pen," point to i card: "Middle sound i-i-i — pin — try again."

If pan becomes long a, model short: "Apple a-a-a in the middle — p-a-n — pan."

If child swaps nip and pin, slow rail slide: "First sound n — nip. First sound p — pin."

Management Cue: Link chart on board: Lesson 14 words (sat/at/tap) ←→ Lesson 15 words (pin/nip/pan).
  1. Model blending rail: pin with slide (≈2 min).
  2. Introduce nip and pan (≈2 min).
  3. Choral rounds × 2 passes + spiral sat/at/tap review (≈2 min).
  4. Mixed blend round — old + new words (≈1 min).
  5. Cards on tray — hands in lap (≈30 sec).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Teacher · i+p+n drill

"Cheer drill! New three — i-i-i insect! P-p-p puff! N-n-n net!"
"Link to old three — sss snake! A-a-a apple! T-t-t tap! All six — stand up!"
"Sss-a-t-i-p-n — Group 1 train — chug chug chug!"

Teacher · Station relay cheer setup

"Six stations around the circle — s, a, t, i, p, n. Walk to your station — do the action — next friend!"
"Station s — snake sss! Station a — apple a-a-a! Station t — tap t-t-t!"
"Station i — insect i-i-i! Station p — puff p-p-p! Station n — net n-n-n!"

Teacher · Team relay close

"Not a race — a team cheer. Everyone finishes — we clap together!"
"Group 1 complete — phonics champions — arms up! Six sounds — one team!"

Teacher · Group 1 song celebration

"Group 1 song! Sss-a-t-i-p-n — sing with me — snake apple tap insect puff net!"
"You finished Group 1 — the first phonics set — I am so proud!"
Handling Mistakes:

If relay becomes racing, pause: "Walking feet — team cheer — not a race."

If a child skips a station, guide gently: "Net station — n-n-n — then next."

Management Cue: Station markers on floor in circle order — prevents line cutting.
  1. i+p+n action drill × 2 rounds (≈2 min).
  2. Full six-sound stand-up cheer linking Lesson 14 (≈2 min).
  3. Team station relay — one loop per child (≈2 min).
  4. Group 1 song audio or teacher chant — celebrate complete set (≈1 min).

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Teacher · Quiet Group 1 review

"Hands on tummy. Whisper — sss — a-a-a — t-t-t — i-i-i — p-p-p — n-n-n."
"Whisper blend — pin — pan — sat — you know so many sounds!"

Teacher · Group 1 celebration

"Group 1 finished — six sounds — s-a-t-i-p-n — I am proud. Stickers — Group 1 star!"
"Show your sticker — Group 1 champion — take it home!"

Teacher · Goodbye song

"Goodbye Song — wave — goodbye [name] — see you again!"
"Next week — sight words — special word friends — exciting!"
Handling Mistakes:

If celebration gets loud, reset whisper chain before Goodbye Song.

  1. Whisper six-sound chain (≈1 min).
  2. Whisper blend review: pin, pan, sat (≈1 min).
  3. Compliment + Group 1 stickers (≈1 min).
  4. Goodbye Song (≈1 min).
  5. Line up exit (≈1 min).
