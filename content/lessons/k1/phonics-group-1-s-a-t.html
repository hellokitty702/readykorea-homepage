K1 Lesson 14: Phonics Group 1 — s, a, t

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes /s/ snake · /a/ apple · /t/ tap · sat · at · tap · CVC blending · Multisensory drill

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Letter cards (3): s · a · t — large lowercase with Jolly Phonics action icons
  • Picture cue cards (3): snake · apple · tap — photo + start sound label
  • CVC word cards (3): sat · at · tap — large print, one word per card
  • Blending rail (1): pocket strip or tape line — s-a-t card slots for sliding blend
  • Sound hoops (3): labeled s / a / t — for picture sort warm-up link
  • Sand tray or finger-paint pad (1): for multisensory trace in Cheering Up
  • Jolly Phonics s-a-t audio clip: speaker or phone (30–45 sec)
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Optional: letter magnets s, a, t for blending rail demo

Learning Objectives

  • Students hear and repeat /s/, /a/, /t/ with Jolly Phonics actions (snake, apple, tap).
  • Students match letter cards s, a, t to picture cues and first-sound prompts.
  • Students blend simple CVC patterns with teacher support: sat, at, tap.
  • Students participate in a full s + a + t multisensory drill — sound, action, trace, and choral blend.
  • Students air-write s, a, t while saying each sound.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Settle into the safe circle, preview Group 1 phonics start, then energize with Jolly actions before CVC blending work.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, preview s-a-t sounds.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, sound explorers! Hello! Find your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "Today we meet three letter sounds — s, a, t!"
"Snake, apple, tap — sss, a-a-a, t-t-t! Are you ready?"

Teacher · Letter card peek

"Peek — s!" (slither hands) "Peek — a!" (apple munch) "Peek — t!" (finger tap)
"Sounds first — letter names later. Listen with your ears — then move your body."

Teacher · Link to Lesson 01 /s/ snake

"Remember Lesson 01? We met snake sound — sssssss! Today snake joins apple and tap — three friends together."
"Show me your snake from Lesson 01 — arms slide — sssssss! Wonderful remembering!"
"Today we squish sounds into words — sat, at, tap — real words you can read!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child says letter names ("ess, ay, tee"), smile: "Fun names! Today — sounds — sss, a-a-a, t-t-t. Copy my snake!"

If a child makes unrelated sounds ("boom!"), redirect: "Big boom! Our sound — sss — quiet snake."

If a child grabs letter cards early, gentle hand: "Cards wait on the tray. Sit on your spot first."

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — wait for whole-group attention before showing letter cards.
Management Cue: Keep blending rail out of reach until Step 3 — build anticipation without distraction.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit with cards — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Flash s, a, t letter cards with one action each (≈1 min).
  4. Preview blending: hold sat card briefly — "Later we squish sounds together — sat!"
  5. Quick hello review — wave Hello to neighbor (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + Jolly Phonics action drill for s, a, t.

Teacher · Command review

"Magic words — Look! Listen! Sit! Wonderful remembering!"
"Now — sound actions! Copy my body every time."

Teacher · Jolly action — S snake

"S — snake! Sssss — slither hands — sssss!" (arms weave like snake)
"Again — watch my mouth — teeth together — air out — sssss!"
"Side A — sss! Side B — sss! Whole class — super snake — sssss!"

Teacher · Jolly action — A apple and T tap

"A — apple! A-a-a — munch munch — a-a-a!" (hand to mouth)
"T — tap! T-t-t — tap tap tap on your knee — t-t-t!"
"All three — sss, a-a-a, t-t-t! Faster — sss-a-t — sat sound coming!"

Teacher · Jolly audio + copy

"Watch the clip — copy exactly. Snake… apple… tap. Your turn — no video — just us!"
"Side A — sss! Side B — a-a-a! Whole class — t-t-t! You are sound stars!"

Teacher · First-sound picture sort

"Three hoops — s, a, t. I hold a picture — you point to the hoop. Snake — which sound?"
"Sss hoop! Apple — a-a-a hoop! Tap picture — t-t-t hoop! You hear first sounds — clever ears!"
Handling Mistakes:

If actions become silly dancing, reset: "Watch my snake — small slither — sss. Copy exactly."

If /a/ becomes long "ay" (letter name), model short: "Tiny apple bite — a-a-a — mouth open small."

If tapping is too loud on desks, redirect: "Tap your knee — soft taps — t-t-t."

Management Cue: Model gesture + sound together every time — never sound-only at K1.
Management Cue: After Jolly clip, use Listen card + 3-second wait before blending setup.
  1. Look · Listen · Sit drill — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  2. Jolly action chain: s → a → t individually, then combined (≈2 min).
  3. Optional Jolly audio clip — children copy actions (≈1 min).
  4. First-sound hoop sort — snake, apple, tap pictures (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "You know s, a, t — now we blend them into words! CVC time!"

Main Activity: CVC Blending & s-a-t Multisensory Drill (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up introduces sat, at, tap with blending rail and cards; Cheering Up runs the full s+a+t multisensory drill — sound, action, sand trace, choral blend. Accept choral blending before individual reading.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — CVC blending with cards: sat, at, tap.

Teacher · Blending rail setup — sat

"Three sounds make a word. Watch the rail — s… a… t. Slide them close — sss-a-t — sat!"
"Say it slow — sss — a — t. Faster — sat! Sat!" (hold sat word card)
"Sat — like I sat on my dot. Everyone — sit on your spot — say sat!"

Teacher · at and tap blends

"Two sounds — a… t — at! At! At my spot — at!" (hold at card)
"Back to three — t… a… p — tap! Tap tap tap!" (hold tap card + knee tap)
"Round two — I point — you blend. s-a-t?" (wait) "Sat! a-t?" (wait) "At! t-a-p?" (wait) "Tap!"

Teacher · Choral blend rounds

"Round three — whisper sat… louder sat… biggest SAT!"
"Girls — at! Boys — tap! Everyone — sat! You are blending champions!"
"Point to the word card — no sounding letter names — sounds only — sat!"

Teacher · Card pass blend

"Hold the sat card — show your neighbor — say sat! Pass — at — tap!"
"If you forget — watch my mouth — sss-a-t — I help you."
"Cards on the tray — blending finished — hands quiet in lap."
Handling Mistakes:

If a child says letter names while blending ("ess-ay-tee"), restart slow: "Sounds only — sss — a — t — sat."

If a child says "sap" for sat, point to cards in order: "Middle sound a — sat — try again."

If blending feels too hard, accept first-sound only: "Great sss! Tomorrow we finish sat together."

Management Cue: Blending rail stays at teacher center for Round 1 — children watch before handling cards in Round 2.
Management Cue: One word at a time — do not introduce extra CVC words beyond sat, at, tap in this lesson.
  1. Teacher models blending rail: s-a-t → sat with slide motion (≈2 min).
  2. Introduce at (2 letters) and tap (3 letters) with same rail (≈2 min).
  3. Choral blend rounds: teacher points — class blends × 3 words × 2 passes (≈2 min).
  4. Optional card pass: children hold and say one word to neighbor (≈1 min).
  5. Cards returned to tray — "Blending finished! Sit on your spot."

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Full s + a + t multisensory drill — sound, action, sand trace, choral blend, peer cheer.

Teacher · Multisensory drill — sound and action

"Sound drill! Four ways — hear it, move it, trace it, say it!"
"Round one — S! Sssss — snake slither! Trace s in the air — sss!"
"Round two — A! A-a-a — apple munch! Round three — T! T-t-t — knee tap!"

Teacher · Multisensory drill — sand trace

"Sand tray time! One finger — one letter — s in the sand — sss!"
"Pass clockwise — a in the sand — a-a-a! t in the sand — t-t-t!"
"Five seconds each — then pass — gentle fingers — no digging!"

Teacher · Full blend cheer and partner cheer

"All three — stand up! Sss — a — t — SAT! Sit down."
"Partner cheer: you do snake sss — friend does apple a-a-a — together tap t-t-t — SAT!"
"Last cheer — at! Tap! Sat! — whole class — phonics champions!"

Teacher · Air-write giant letters

"Stand up — giant air-write! S — start up, curve, sss! A — circle, stick, a-a-a! T — across, down, t-t-t!"
"Sit down — whisper — sat — at — tap. Beautiful air-writing!"
Handling Mistakes:

If sand trace becomes play, reset: "One finger — one letter — pass tray to friend."

If partner cheer is off-task, assign shoulder partners and demo once seated.

If /s/ is shouted, whisper round: "Tiny snake sss — then we cheer big."

Management Cue: Sand tray passes one direction only — clockwise — to prevent tray grabs.
Management Cue: Stand up / sit down counts "3, 2, 1" every time during drill cheers.
  1. Sound + action drill: s, a, t individually × 2 rounds (≈2 min).
  2. Sand or finger-paint trace: one letter per child pass (≈2 min).
  3. Stand-up choral blend: sat, at, tap × 3 cheers (≈1 min).
  4. Partner cheer: sss + a-a-a + t-t-t = sat (≈1 min).
  5. Air-write giant s, a, t while saying sounds — final check (≈1 min).

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Lower energy with quiet sounds, affirm blending effort, air-write review, Goodbye Song.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Teacher · Breathing & quiet sounds

"Phonics time is finished. Hands on your tummy."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… sss, quiet snake. Again — out… t-t-t, soft tap."
"Whisper blend — sat. Whisper — at. Whisper — tap."

Teacher · Compliment praise frames

"Today you made sss, a-a-a, t-t-t. You blended sat! I am proud of you."
"I am proud of [name] for brave blending. I am proud of [name] for quiet snake sounds."
"Everyone gets a sticker. Line up — quiet feet."

Teacher · Goodbye song & dismissal

"Stand up slowly. Stretch — big arms up! Goodbye Song time. Wave your hands."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone — see you again!"
"High five at the door. Bye bye! Next week — more sounds!"
Handling Mistakes:

If whisper round becomes shouting, hold Listen card — "Tiny sounds only — sss — try again."

If a child refuses sticker, offer later: "Sticker waits here — you did great work."

Management Cue: Compliment 2–3 children by name — note blend attempts, not perfection.
  1. Two breath cycles with quiet /s/ and /t/ (≈1 min).
  2. Whisper blend review: sat, at, tap (≈1 min).
  3. Compliment + stickers (≈1 min).
  4. Goodbye Song with name verse (≈1 min).
  5. Line up, high-five exit (≈1 min).
