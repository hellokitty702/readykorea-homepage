K1 Lesson 09: Action Verbs Jump & Run

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Jump · Run · Walk · Sit · Stand · Can you ___? · /j/ jump · /r/ run

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Action verb cards (5): jump · run · walk · sit · stand — photo + word, large
  • Obstacle mini-course markers (5–6): cones, tape lines, or carpet squares — jump line, walk line, sit spot
  • Go / Stop sign cards (2): green go · red stop — for course control
  • Phonics flashcards: jump picture + /j/ jump · run picture + /r/ run
  • Action song audio: speaker or phone (30–45 sec freeze-dance clip)
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Hello / Goodbye cards: brief greeting from Lesson 02
  • Circle seating: floor dots — return spot after each course round
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Optional: soft mat strip for jump landing zone — safer landings at K1

Learning Objectives

  • Students perform five action verbs: jump, run, walk, sit, stand on command.
  • Students respond to "Can you ___?" with action — full sentence not required at K1.
  • Students complete a short action verb obstacle mini-course — walk, jump, sit, stand in sequence.
  • Students freeze on Stop and move on Go during course rounds.
  • Students hear and respond to /j/ (jump) and /r/ (run) with picture cards and body TPR.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Channel high motor energy safely, preview action words, then build verb fluency before the obstacle mini-course.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, preview action theme with clear space rules.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, friends! Hello! Welcome back. Sit on your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "Today we move our body — jump, run, walk!"
"Walking feet in the circle. Running comes later — on the course only. Are you ready?"

Teacher · Door greeting script

"Hello, [name]! Welcome back — walking feet today!"
"Find your blue dot. Sit first — big jumps come on the obstacle course later."
"[Name] is here — one more friend — our action team is ready!"

Teacher · Action card peek

"Peek — jump!" (flash jump card, small hop in place) "Peek — run!" (flash run card, jog in place)
"Sit and Stand are magic control words. We sit in circle — we stand when teacher says Stand up in 3, 2, 1."
"Save big jumps for the course — listen first!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child runs into circle at arrival, pause: "Walking feet to your dot. Running is for the course — sit first."

If a child jumps on Sit command, model without scolding: "Sit looks like this — bottom down. Copy me."

If a child says "Fly!", redirect: "Birds fly! Today — jump, run, walk — pick one when I ask."

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — establish stop/go rules before any standing TPR in Meeting Up.
Management Cue: Define course boundary with tape before children arrive — "Stay inside the lines — walking feet between stations."
Management Cue: Clear center circle of obstacles until Step 3 — prevents tripping during warm-up sit time.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Flash jump and run cards — model small in-place motion (≈1 min).
  4. State space rules: course only for run; freeze on Stop card (≈30 sec).
  5. Preview mini-course path with finger on floor (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + action verb drill with freeze-dance contrast before obstacle course.

Teacher · Greeting + command review

"Hello, friends! How are you? I'm happy! Thumbs up!"
"Look! Listen! Sit! Magic words — always!"

Teacher · Action freeze-dance

"Stand up in 3, 2, 1! Jump!" (small hops) "Sit!" (freeze down)
"Stand! Run in place — fast feet!" (jog) "Stop!" (hold Stop card — freeze)
"Walk — slow slow feet. Sit! Stand! Jump once — Sit! You control your body!"

Teacher · Can you ___? chorus

"Can you jump?" (class jumps) "Can you run?" (jog in place)
"Can you walk?" (slow steps) "Can you sit?" (sit) "Can you stand?" (stand)
"Sit for circle — course time next!"
Handling Mistakes:

If freeze-dance becomes chaos, drop music and use cards only: "Stop card — freeze. Go card — jump once."

If a child runs around the circle, point to course tape: "Run on the line only — come back to your dot."

If a child won't stand for jump, offer seated bounce: "Bounce in your spot — jump from sitting — brave!"

Management Cue: Always pair Stand up in 3, 2, 1 with Sit down in 3, 2, 1 — same countdown every time prevents stampede.
Management Cue: Stop card at chest height — hold 3 full seconds — no talking during freeze; builds course safety habit.
Management Cue: Half-class moves during freeze-dance if space tight — seated half watches, then swap.
  1. Hello + Look · Listen · Sit — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  2. Action freeze-dance: jump, run, walk, sit, stand × 2 cycles (≈2 min).
  3. Can you ___? chorus — full verb set once (≈1 min).
  4. Return to sit — hands in lap — 3-second Listen wait (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "You know the verbs — obstacle mini-course — GO!"

Teacher · Transition to Main Activity

"Warm-up finished! You know jump, run, walk, sit, stand."
"Look at me. Listen. Sit. Hands in lap — obstacle course is next!"
"Remember — Stop card means freeze. Go card means move. Walking feet to the start line!"

Main Activity: Action Obstacle Mini-Course & /j/ /r/ Phonics (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up builds verb fluency through a short sequenced course; Cheering Up locks /j/ jump and /r/ run through picture cards and body TPR. One child on course at a time or pairs max at K1.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — action verb obstacle mini-course: walk to line, jump, sit, stand, run back.

Teacher · Course setup

"Obstacle mini-course! Station one — walk to the tape. Station two — jump! Station three — sit. Station four — stand. Station five — run back to your dot!"
"One friend at a time — or two — walking feet waiting. Go card means go — Stop card means freeze!"
"Can you jump? Can you run? Show me on the course!"

Teacher · Model lap

"Watch me. Walk… jump… sit… stand… run back! Slow motion first."
"Your turn, Minho! Walk — jump — sit — stand — run! Friends cheer — Jump! Run!"
"Round two — new verb order! Walk — run — sit — stand — jump back! Listen for my words!"

Teacher · Cheer squad script

"Seated friends are the cheer squad! When Minho jumps, you say — Jump! When Sora runs, you say — Run!"
"Cheer squad rules — sit on your dot, quiet voice, kind words only."
"Can you jump? Can you run? — we ask the runner, cheer squad answers together!"

Teacher · Course rounds

"Round one — full course each child. Round two — half class goes, half cheers Can you ___?"
"Stop! Freeze! (Stop card) Go! (Go card) Last runner — gentle run — high five!"
"Course finished — sit on your spot. Walking feet only!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child skips a station, gently redirect: "Jump station — one jump — then sit. Try again — I walk with you."

If running becomes unsafe, shrink course: "Run to the cone only — return walk. Small run today."

If a child won't wait turn, give job: "You hold the Go card — only you say Go!"

If collision at jump line, reset: "One jumper — others sit and cheer. Line order — same as circle."

Management Cue: One child on course at a time for first round — K1 safety; pair only if space is wide and children know Stop rule.
Management Cue: Seated children chant verb only — "Jump! Run!" — keeps all engaged without wandering into course path.
Management Cue: Co-teacher or assistant at jump station — hands ready to spot landings at K1.
  1. Teacher models full course slow motion (≈1 min).
  2. Round 1: each child completes walk-jump-sit-stand-run sequence (≈4 min).
  3. Round 2 (optional): varied verb order or half-class cheer round (≈2 min).
  4. Stop/Go drill mid-course once — freeze practice (≈30 sec).
  5. All return to circle dots — cones pushed to edge (≈30 sec).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Phonics /j/ jump and /r/ run — picture cards + body TPR + peer cheer.

Teacher · Introduce /j/ jump

"Jump starts with J! J says j-j-jump. Jaw drop — juh-juh-juh. Jump — j-j-jump!"
"This is jump." (show jump card) "Jump TPR — bend knees — BOING — juh — jump!"
"Stand up in 3, 2, 1 — one jump — juh! Sit down — quiet juh."

Teacher · Introduce /r/ run + partner cheer

"Run starts with R! R says r-r-run. Lip buzz — rrr-rrr-rrr. Run — r-r-run!"
"This is run." (show run card) "Run TPR — fast feet in place — rrr — run!"
"Partner cheer: you jump — juh! Friend runs in place — rrr! Switch!"

Teacher · Praise frames

"Wow! I heard juh — jump sound!"
"Super run sound — rrr-rrr!"
"Your BOING jump is perfect — juh!"
"High five for fast feet — rrr — run!"

Teacher · Body TPR check

"Jump card — bend — BOING — juh! Run card — fast feet — rrr!"
"Stand — jump once — juh! Sit — run feet in place — rrr! Stay in your spot."
"Last check — hold both cards — jump or run? Listen and move!"
Handling Mistakes:

If /j/ sounds like /d/ ("dump"), model slow: "Juh — jaw open — jump. Watch my mouth."

If /r/ is /w/ ("wun"), accept at K1: "Great fast feet! Rrr — lip buzz — run!"

If partner cheer becomes racing across circle, reset: "Run in place — feet fast — stay on your dot."

If jumps get too big, limit: "Small jump — boing — safe landing — juh!"

Management Cue: Stand up in 3, 2, 1 / Sit down in 3, 2, 1 — every phonics TPR round; non-negotiable after obstacle course.
Management Cue: Shoulder partners for cheer — in-place run only, no lap running during phonics block.
Management Cue: Praise in-place run as hard as lap run — K1 needs success without extra space.
  1. Show jump card — model /j/ × 3, one small jump TPR × 3.
  2. Show run card — model /r/ × 3, in-place run TPR × 3.
  3. Alternating drill: teacher holds jump or run card — class responds with action + sound.
  4. Partner cheer: 30 seconds each side — jump juh / run rrr exchange.
  5. Whole-group check: jump card → juh + hop; run card → rrr + fast feet.

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Lower heart rate after obstacle course, affirm safe movement, dismiss with Goodbye Song.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Purpose: Breathing, compliment, goodbye song, dismissal.

Teacher · Calm breathing

"Action time is finished. Sit on your spot. Hands in lap — quiet body."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… juh, 1, 2, 3. Soft jump breath out."
"Again. In… 1, 2, 3. Out… rrr… quiet run breath. Feet still — finished."

Teacher · Compliment circle

"Today you jumped and ran on the obstacle course! You froze on Stop. You made juh and rrr sounds."
"I am proud of [name] for safe Stop freeze. I am proud of [name] for gentle jumping."
"I am proud of [name] for kind cheering. Everyone gets a sticker."

Teacher · Goodbye routine

"Stand up slowly. Stretch — reach high — then still body."
"Goodbye Song time. Wave your hands."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye, goodbye, see you again."
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone — see you again!"
"Line up — walking feet only — high five at the door. Bye bye!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child keeps running during breathing, hold Stop card: "Stop — statue — in… out… walking feet later."

If line-up stampede, pause song: "Sit one more second — then line — you can do it."

If a child is overstimulated, quiet door compliment + water if school allows — no forced high-five.

Management Cue: Compliment 2–3 children by name — include one Stop/freeze praise after motor lesson.
Management Cue: Dismiss in table order — walking feet only — never run to door after action lesson.
Management Cue: Put Go/Stop cards away visibly before Goodbye Song — signals motor time is closed.
  1. Two breath cycles with /j/ and /r/ exhale cues (≈1 min).
  2. Compliments + stickers — name 2–3 course moments (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye Song with individual name verse (≈2 min).
  4. Line up — high-five exit — walking feet (≈1 min).
