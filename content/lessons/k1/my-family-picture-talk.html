K1 Lesson 08: My Family Picture Talk

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes Mom · Dad · Brother · Sister · Baby · This is my ___ · /m/ mom · /d/ dad

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Family member cards (5): mom · dad · brother · sister · baby — diverse photo + word, large
  • Sample family poster (1): teacher's sample or generic family — for circle model only
  • Family puppet (1): hand puppet for shy sharers — "This is my mom!" via puppet
  • Circle share photo cards (6–8): laminated generic family scenes — child picks one to talk about
  • Phonics flashcards: mom picture + /m/ mom · dad picture + /d/ dad
  • Sentence frame card (1): "This is my ___." — visible during share round
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Hello / Goodbye cards: brief greeting from Lesson 02
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down
  • Optional: small photo from home in sealed envelope — only if families opted in; never require personal photos

Learning Objectives

  • Students name five family members: mom, dad, brother, sister, baby.
  • Students point to a picture and say "This is my ___" with one family word (word or gesture accepted).
  • Students listen respectfully during classmates' family shares — quiet hands, eyes on speaker.
  • Students participate in family puppet circle share with puppet support when needed.
  • Students hear and respond to /m/ (mom) and /d/ (dad) with picture cards and hug TPR.

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Create a warm, non-invasive entry to family talk, then preview vocabulary before circle share builds speaking confidence.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Psychological safety, predictable arrival, gentle preview of family theme.

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, friends! Hello! Welcome back. Sit on your spot — same safe circle."
"Look at me." (hold Look card) "Listen." (hold Listen card) "Today we talk about family — people who love us."
"Mom, dad, brother, sister, baby. All families are different — all families are special."

Teacher · Door greeting script

"Hello, [name]! I am so happy you came today."
"You may wave or say Hello — both are perfect. Find your dot — same circle as always."
"[Name] is here — let's count friends. One, two, three… we are a family circle today!"

Teacher · Family card peek

"Peek — mom!" (flash mom card) "Peek — dad!" (flash dad card)
"We use kind words today. We listen when friends share. No questions about home — only happy labels."
"[Puppet name] has a family too — mom and dad! Say hello, [puppet name]!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child asks invasive questions ("Where is your dad?"), redirect: "We only say This is my ___. Kind labels today."

If a child says "I have no brother," validate: "Some families have sisters only — or pets! Pick any card word that fits you."

If a child becomes emotional about family, offer puppet or pass: "You can whisper to [puppet name] or listen today — both are brave."

Management Cue: Eyes on me in 3, 2, 1 — establish circle calm before any family vocabulary — sensitive topic needs extra safety.
Management Cue: Post sentence frame card visible all lesson: "This is my ___." — reduces pressure to invent new language.
Management Cue: Never require home photos — generic laminated cards and puppet talk are full participation for every child.
  1. Door greeting: Hello to each child + invite to circle spot.
  2. Look · Listen · Sit — 3-second settle wait.
  3. Flash mom and dad cards — model "This is my mom" with sample poster (≈1 min).
  4. Puppet intro: puppet says "This is my dad!" — children wave to puppet (≈30 sec).
  5. State listening rule: "When a friend shares, we look and listen — quiet hands."

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Purpose: Energy + family word chorus with hug gestures before individual circle share.

Teacher · Greeting + command review

"Hello, friends! How are you? I'm happy! Thumbs up!"
"Look! Listen! Sit! Magic words — you remember!"

Teacher · Family word chorus

"Mom!" (arms open hug) "Dad!" (hug gesture) "Brother!" (high-five gesture)
"Sister!" (hold hands gesture) "Baby!" (rocking arms) "Family hug — arms around yourself — love!"
"Your turn — copy my gesture. Mom — hug! Dad — hug! Baby — rock rock!"

Teacher · Quick label drill

"Who is this?" (hold mom card) "Mom!"
"Who is this?" (hold sister card) "Sister! This is my sister!"
"One more — baby! This is my baby! Rock rock!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child uses L1 family word only, accept and echo in English: "엄마 — yes — mom! This is my mom!"

If a child laughs at another's share word, firm but kind: "Kind circle. We listen — no silly voices during share."

If hug gesture becomes rough hugging neighbors, reset: "Hug yourself — gentle family hug — arms around you."

Management Cue: Model hug gesture on self only during warm-up — prevents grabbing classmates before share rules are set.
Management Cue: Use diverse family cards — single-parent, grandparent, two-dad/two-mom images if available — normalize all families quietly.
Management Cue: After family chorus, Listen card + 3-second wait — transition from loud chorus to quiet share circle.
  1. Hello + Look · Listen · Sit — 2 rounds (≈1 min).
  2. Family word chorus with gesture × full set of five (≈2 min).
  3. Quick label drill: teacher holds card, class says word + sentence frame once (≈1 min).
  4. Listening rule reminder: "Eyes on speaker — quiet hands" (≈30 sec).
  5. Transition: "Now real pictures — family puppet circle share!"

Teacher · Transition to Main Activity

"Warm-up finished! You know mom, dad, brother, sister, baby."
"Look at me. Listen. Sit. Quiet hands — family puppet circle is next!"
"[Puppet name] is ready. When friends share, we listen with kind eyes. Are you ready?"

Main Activity: Family Puppet Circle Share & /m/ /d/ Phonics (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up builds speaking through low-pressure family puppet circle share; Cheering Up locks /m/ mom and /d/ dad through picture cards and hug TPR. Never ask invasive family questions.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Purpose: MAIN theme activity — family puppet circle share: each child holds a card or photo and says "This is my ___."

Teacher · Circle share setup

"Family puppet circle! I give you one picture card. Look — find mom or dad or sister — whoever you want."
"When it is your turn, hold up your card. Say: This is my mom! Or point and say one word — mom! Both are perfect."
"Friends listen — quiet hands — kind eyes on the speaker."

Teacher · Model + puppet round

"Watch me. This is my mom!" (hold sample poster) "Hug gesture — love!"
"[Puppet name]'s turn. This is my dad!" (puppet voice) "Your turn, Sora!"
"If you want help, [puppet name] whispers with you — brave share!"

Teacher · Puppet circle listening round

"Puppet circle rule — when [friend's name] talks, we look and listen. No calling out — quiet hands."
"[Puppet name] listens too — see? Eyes on the speaker. Ears open."
"After each share we echo together — This is my mom! — one time only. Kind voices — family love!"

Teacher · Share rounds

"Round one — mom or dad only. Round two — brother, sister, or baby. Round three — pick your favorite family word again!"
"Class echoes: This is my mom! Nice share, Minho!"
"No questions — only clap and smile. Family circle finished — gentle clap!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child freezes, offer puppet co-voice: "Puppet says it with you — This is my… dad!" Accept one word or gesture.

If a child shares wrong label ("This is my dog"), smile: "Dogs are family too! Today use mom, dad, brother, sister, or baby — pick one."

If a child cries during share, pause circle: "Feelings are OK. You can listen today. [Puppet name] sits with you."

If share runs long, fast round: point to card + one word only — no full sentence required.

Management Cue: Pass cards in order around circle — predictable turn order reduces anxiety; skip is always allowed with a nod.
Management Cue: Echo phrase after each share — whole class repeats "This is my mom!" — builds community without putting shy child on spot twice.
Management Cue: Co-teacher or puppet models every round before first child share — K1 needs multiple models for sentence frames.
  1. Distribute laminated family scene cards — one per child (≈1 min).
  2. Teacher + puppet model full share (≈1 min).
  3. Round 1: each child shares mom or dad — word or sentence (≈3 min).
  4. Round 2: swap card or pick new member — brother/sister/baby (≈2 min).
  5. Class echo + gentle clap after each share; collect cards in basket (≈1 min).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Purpose: Phonics /m/ mom and /d/ dad — picture cards + hug TPR + peer cheer.

Teacher · Introduce /m/ mom

"Mom starts with M! M says m-m-mom. Lips together — mmm-mmm-mmm. Mom — mmm!"
"This is mom." (show mom card) "Family hug — arms open — mmm — mom!"
"Stand up! Hug yourself — mmm — mom! Sit down — quiet mmm."

Teacher · Introduce /d/ dad + partner cheer

"Dad starts with D! D says d-d-dad. Tongue tap — duh-duh-duh. Dad — duh!"
"This is dad." (show dad card) "Strong hug — duh — dad! Tap chest — d-d-dad!"
"Partner cheer: you are mom — mmm hug! Friend is dad — duh tap! Switch!"

Teacher · Praise frames

"Wow! I heard mmm — mom sound!"
"Super dad sound — duh-duh!"
"Your hug is perfect — mmm — family love!"
"High five for listening — you know mom and dad sounds!"

Teacher · Hug TPR check

"Mom card — hug — mmm! Dad card — tap chest — duh!"
"Stand up — mom — mmm! Sit — dad — duh! In your spot."
"Last check — hold both cards — mom or dad? Listen and hug or tap!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child confuses mmm and duh, show cards side by side: "Mom — mmm — hug. Dad — duh — tap."

If hug TPR becomes grabbing a partner, reset: "Hug yourself — mmm — own body hug only."

If a child won't do partner cheer, teacher pairs: "You are my mom — mmm! I am dad — duh!"

If /m/ has no voice, hand on throat: "Feel the buzz — mmm — mom!"

Management Cue: Before standing TPR, say "Stand up in 3, 2, 1" and "Sit down in 3, 2, 1" every time — predictable transitions.
Management Cue: Shoulder partners for cheer — "Turn to your neighbor" reduces crossing the circle.
Management Cue: Accept mom/dad word without clear /m/ or /d/ — praise hug gesture loudly at K1.
  1. Show mom card — model /m/ × 3, hug TPR × 3.
  2. Show dad card — model /d/ × 3, chest tap TPR × 3.
  3. Alternating drill: teacher holds mom or dad card — class responds with sound + gesture.
  4. Partner cheer: 30 seconds each side — mom mmm / dad duh exchange.
  5. Whole-group check: mom card → mmm + hug; dad card → duh + tap.

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Purpose: Close family talk warmly, affirm shares, dismiss with Goodbye Song and family hug ritual.

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Purpose: Breathing, compliment, goodbye song, dismissal.

Teacher · Calm breathing

"Family time is finished. Sit on your spot. Hands in lap — quiet circle."
"Breathe in… 1, 2, 3. Breathe out… mmm, 1, 2, 3. Soft mom breath out."
"Again. In… 1, 2, 3. Out… duh… quiet dad breath. Family hug — arms around you — still."

Teacher · Compliment circle

"Today you shared your family in our puppet circle! You said This is my mom and dad. You made mmm and duh sounds."
"I am proud of [name] for a brave puppet share. I am proud of [name] for kind listening eyes."
"I am proud of [name] for a gentle family hug. Everyone gets a sticker — quiet feet."

Teacher · Goodbye routine

"Stand up slowly. Stretch — big arms — family hug around yourself."
"Goodbye Song time. Wave your hands."
"Goodbye, goodbye, see you again. Goodbye, goodbye, see you again."
"Goodbye [name], goodbye [name], goodbye everyone — see you again!"
"Line up at the door. High five — bye bye! Tell your family — This is my mom!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child is still emotional after family share, quiet compliment at door: "You listened bravely today — sticker for kind heart."

If family hug circle gets too tight, open to self-hug only: "Hug yourself — love — gentle arms."

If line-up rush, pause song: "Walking feet — same order — family waits together."

Management Cue: Compliment 2–3 children by name — include one listener praise, not only speakers.
Management Cue: Dismiss in table order — predictable exit after emotional content helps regulation.
Management Cue: End with self-hug + wave — no forced group hug at K1 if any child showed distress during share.
  1. Two breath cycles with /m/ and /d/ exhale cues (≈1 min).
  2. Compliments + stickers — name 2–3 share or listen moments (≈1 min).
  3. Goodbye Song with individual name verse (≈2 min).
  4. Line up — high-five exit ritual (≈1 min).
