K1 Lesson 16: Sight Words Round 1

Grade: K1 (Ages 3-4) Time: 30 minutes a · I · the · see · go · Word Wall Game · Sight Word Slap · Visual Recognition

Material Checklist — Full Lesson

  • Sight word cards (5): a · I · the · see · go — large print, one word per card
  • Word wall strip (1): pocket chart or wall space for five words in fixed order
  • Slap cards (5 sets): duplicate words spread on floor mat for slap game
  • Soft fly swatters (2): foam only — for controlled slap game
  • Sentence frame strip (1): "I see the ___" with blank picture slot
  • Picture cards (4): dog · sun · ball · cat — for sentence frame fill-in
  • Mini flash ring blanks (optional): 1 per child for take-home exposure
  • Look · Listen · Sit cards: review from Lesson 01
  • Listen pause card (1): red stop-hand icon
  • Circle seating: floor dots — one spot per child
  • Sticker sheet: 1 per child for Calming Down

Learning Objectives

  • Students recognize five sight words by shape: a, I, the, see, go.
  • Students point to the correct word on the word wall when the teacher calls it.
  • Students participate in a word wall game — find, point, and choral read.
  • Students play sight word slap with visual cards — tap the word heard, not sounded out.
  • Students build one simple sentence frame: "I see the ___."

Warm-up: Meeting Up & Warming Up (10 mins)

Purpose: Settle into circle, preview sight words as special whole-word friends, energize with word shape peek game before word wall work.

Step 1 · Meeting Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · Arrival & circle welcome

"Good morning, word watchers! Hello! Same safe circle — sit on your dot."
"Look · Listen · Sit. Today we meet special words — sight words! We see them — we know them — no sounding needed."
"Five friends: a, I, the, see, go. Are you ready?"

Teacher · Sight word concept

"Some words hide sounds — tricky words! We learn them by looking — like a face you know."
"This is I — tall letter — I! This is a — small round friend — a!"
"We point — we read — we cheer. Listen first!"

Teacher · Contrast phonics vs sight words

"Last weeks — we sounded sss-a-t — sat. Sight words are different — the whole word is one picture."
"The — we don't say t-h-e slowly — we say the — one shape — like a smile you recognize!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child tries to sound out "the" letter by letter, redirect gently: "Sight word — look at the whole shape — the!"

If a child says "eye" for I, clarify: "Big letter I — me — I see!"

Management Cue: Word wall covered with cloth until Step 3 reveal — builds anticipation.
  1. Door greeting + circle settle with Look · Listen · Sit.
  2. Explain sight word concept — whole-word recognition (≈1 min).
  3. Flash I and a cards once each — "Look at the shape!"
  4. Preview word wall game — "Later we hunt words on the wall!"
  5. Hello wave to neighbor (≈30 sec).

Step 2 · Warming Up (≈5 min)

Teacher · Shape peek drill

"Peek game! I show a word fast — you say it! Peek — I! Peek — a! Peek — the!"
"Peek — see! Peek — go! Again — slower — I… see… go!"
"Point to your eyes — see! Point to yourself — I! Point to the door — go!"

Teacher · a vs I shape contrast

"Tricky pair — a and I! Side by side — small round a — tall straight I!"
"Point to a — round! Point to I — tall! Wonderful shape eyes!"

Teacher · Choral read chain

"All five together — I, a, the, see, go! Whisper first… louder… biggest cheer!"
"Magic words — Look! Listen! Sit! Then — word wall game!"

Teacher · Gesture link drill

"See — point to eyes! Go — point to door! I — point to yourself! The — point to something in the room!"
"A — hold up one finger — one small word — a!"
Handling Mistakes:

If peek game becomes shouting random words, reset with Listen card — "Eyes on the card shape only."

If a child confuses a and I, hold side by side: "Small a — round. Tall I — straight. Point to I!"

Management Cue: Peek flashes max 2 seconds each — prevents sounding-out attempts.
  1. Look · Listen · Sit drill — 1 round (≈30 sec).
  2. Shape peek drill — five words × 2 passes (≈2 min).
  3. a vs I contrast — side by side (≈30 sec).
  4. Gesture link: see → eyes, I → self, go → door (≈1 min).
  5. Choral read chain — whisper to loud (≈1 min).

Main Activity: Word Wall Game & Sight Word Slap (15 mins)

Teacher Note: Opening Up builds word wall pointing fluency; Cheering Up runs sight word slap with visual cards on the floor. K1 sight words = exposure + recognition, not spelling tests.

Step 3 · Opening Up (≈8 min)

Teacher · Word wall reveal

"Cloth off — ta-da! Word wall! Five words live here all week. Same place — same shape — every day."
"Top row — I, a. Bottom row — the, see, go. Look at every shape — 5 seconds — look!"

Teacher · Word wall game — Round 1

"I say a word — you point to the wall — we read together. Ready — point to I!"
"Point to a! Point to the! Point to see! Point to go!"
"Read with me — I — a — the — see — go!"

Teacher · Word wall game — mixed rounds

"Round two — mix order — go! the! I! a! see!"
"Round three — faster — see! go! I! the! a! You found every word — word wall champions!"

Teacher · Sentence frame — I see the ___

"Sentence frame on the board — I… see… the… dog! I see the dog!" (add picture)
"I see the sun! I see the ball! I see the cat! You read the special words — I, see, the!"
"Your turn — choral — I see the dog! I see the sun! Point to each sight word on the frame!"

Teacher · Volunteer pointer

"One helper — pointer wand — find go on the wall. Everyone reads — go!"
"Helper two — find the — everyone — the! Thank you, helpers. Sit on your spot — slap game next!"
Handling Mistakes:

If a child points to wrong word, model without shame: "Watch the shape — t-h-e — the. Point with me."

If a child sounds out "see" as s-e-e, redirect: "Sight word — whole word — see! Like a picture."

If volunteer pointer freezes, offer wand together: "We point together — see — perfect!"

Management Cue: Word wall word positions stay fixed all week — never rearrange mid-lesson.
  1. Reveal word wall — place five cards in fixed order (≈1 min).
  2. Round 1: teacher calls — class points + choral read (≈2 min).
  3. Round 2–3: mixed order calls × 2 passes (≈2 min).
  4. Sentence frame: I see the + picture cards × 4 (≈2 min).
  5. Two volunteer pointers with wand (≈1 min).

Step 4 · Cheering Up (≈7 min)

Teacher · Slap game setup

"Slap game! Words on the mat — soft swatters — gentle tap only!"
"I say see — you tap see! No slap friends — tap the card!"
"Two helpers with swatters — everyone else points from their spot."

Teacher · Slap rounds — slow pass

"Tap — the! Tap — go! Tap — I! Tap — a! Tap — see!"
"Everyone point from your spot — the — go — I — a — see — wonderful pointing!"

Teacher · Slap rounds — fast pass

"Fast round — I say — you tap or point — go! the! see! I! a!"
"Again — faster — a! I! go! see! the! You are sight word superstars!"

Teacher · Partner point round

"Swatters rest. Partner point — neighbor points to go on the mat — go!"
"Partner says a word — you point — I — see — the — team pointing!"
"Slap game finished — sit on your spot — hands in lap!"
Handling Mistakes:

If swatters hit people, pause game: "Cards only — soft tap. Swatters rest — point with finger."

If two children tap different words, hold both cards: "Listen again — the — everyone tap the."

If a child taps randomly, slow down: "Wait for my word — eyes on me — then tap."

Management Cue: Only 2 swatters active — all others point from seated circle to reduce chaos.
  1. Lay five slap cards on center mat — model gentle tap (≈1 min).
  2. Teacher-call slow rounds — two swatter helpers × 5 words (≈2 min).
  3. Fast mixed-order round × 2 passes (≈2 min).
  4. Seated partner point — no swatters (≈1 min).
  5. Collect swatters — "Slap game finished — sit on your spot."

Wrap-up: Calming Down (5 mins)

Step 5 · Calming Down (≈5 min)

Teacher · Quiet word review

"Hands on tummy. Whisper — I — a — the — see — go."
"Whisper sentence — I see the cat. You know five special words!"

Teacher · Compliment & flash ring

"I am proud of [name] for brave pointing. I am proud of [name] for gentle slapping."
"Optional flash ring for home — show parents — no test — just look!"

Teacher · Goodbye song

"Goodbye Song — wave — goodbye [name] — see you again!"
"Practice I see the ___ at home with a picture book!"
Handling Mistakes:

If flash ring assembly takes too long, send home pre-made rings next lesson — do not rush goodbye.

  1. Whisper five-word chain (≈1 min).
  2. Whisper sentence frame review (≈1 min).
  3. Compliment + optional flash ring (≈1 min).
  4. Stickers + Goodbye Song (≈1 min).
  5. Line up + high-five exit (≈1 min).
